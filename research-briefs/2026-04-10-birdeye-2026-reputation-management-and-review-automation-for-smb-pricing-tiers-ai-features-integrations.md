# Birdeye 2026 — Reputation Management & Review Automation for SMB

## Summary

Birdeye has evolved from a review-management tool into a full-cycle "Agentic Marketing Platform" for local and multi-location businesses. Trusted by over 200,000 businesses, Birdeye helps brands grow their local presence, manage their reputation, and enhance customer experiences, operating globally with teams in the U.S., U.K., Australia, and India. Its 2026 product suite centers on autonomous AI agents (BirdAI) that handle reviews, listings, social media, messaging, and — most recently — Generative Engine Optimization via Search AI. Pricing starts at roughly $299/month per location (Starter) and scales to $449–$499+/month (Dominate), with annual contracts standard and per-location discounts for multi-site brands. The platform integrates with 3,000+ systems and is strongest for mid-market multi-location businesses, though its cost and opaque pricing remain friction points for smaller single-location SMBs.

## Key Findings

### Product & Positioning
- Birdeye is built as an agentic marketing platform for multi-location brands. Instead of organizing capabilities into separate functions, Birdeye connects discovery, reputation, engagement, and conversion into a single system powered by AI agents.
- In G2's Spring 2026 reports, Birdeye ranked as the #1 enterprise platform across 12 categories, backed by 3,600+ verified reviews, including recognition in both core reputation categories and newer AI categories like Agentic AI and AI Agents.

### Pricing Tiers (2026)
- **Starter Plan** ($299/month, 1 location, unlimited users): a solid choice for small local businesses looking to manage online reviews and maintain accurate business listings. It offers core reputation management tools — review collection, automated requests, and multi-channel messaging. However, management of social media platforms and AI chatbot features are missing.
- **Growth Plan** (~$349/month per location): Multi-location businesses or enterprises looking for advanced features can opt for Growth. At $349 per location per month, it's an investment — especially for review generation and automation. In addition to everything in Starter, the Growth plan adds more advanced text messaging, review campaigns, and customer interaction tools.
- **Dominate Plan** ($449/month per location, unlimited users): made for agencies or consultants managing multiple clients. This plan includes Birdeye's full feature set, such as advanced review campaigns, chatbot capabilities, enhanced messaging, and robust listings management.
- Annual contracts are standard (12 months). Month-to-month exists at 40% higher prices ($419 instead of $299 for Starter). Businesses should be aware of set up fees for complex CRM or POS integrations, and strict annual contracts with auto-renewal that require written cancellation 90 days before renewal.
- For businesses with 4 or more locations, the listed Starter/Growth/Dominate plans are replaced by a customizable "Premium" plan that requires direct consultation with the Birdeye sales team.

### AI Features (BirdAI)
- BirdAI blends proprietary brand and industry LLM models with an agentic AI stack. AI Agents draft, publish, and optimize brand-aligned content across social, reviews, and messaging.
- **Reviews AI**: Birdeye Reviews AI automates the process of soliciting reviews through customizable email and SMS templates that reach customers at the perfect moment. Its smart targeting capability identifies which customers are most likely to leave positive feedback. Response Automation uses AI to generate timely responses to reviews, fostering quick engagement with customers.
- **Search AI** (launched Sept 2025): a first-of-its-kind platform to help multi-location brands manage GEO (Generative Engine Optimization), powered by BirdAI. Also introducing the Search Optimization Agent, now in closed beta, which continuously analyzes prompts, citations, and AI-surfaced answers, and can autonomously optimize a brand's GEO with one-click approvals.
- **Insights AI**: Provides actionable insights from sentiment analysis, helping businesses understand and improve their online reputation. Benchmarking Tools compare your business performance against competitors.
- Birdeye supports human-in-the-loop approvals for any AI output, such as review replies, social posts, or survey messages. With BrandAI, every AI agent operates within brand-specific guardrails that you define.

### Integrations
- Birdeye integrates with over 3,000 software systems — CRM, EMR/PMS, POS, property management, marketing, and payments — to make it easy to automatically get new reviews, be found online, improve operations, and increase revenue.
- Notable integrations include Google Workspace, Salesforce, Zendesk, Slack, and HubSpot, among others.
- The platform supports modern REST APIs, webhooks, SSO, role-based permissions, and secure exports to your data cloud.
- Some users report that not all integrations are seamless. For example, partial integrations with tools like FieldEdge require additional steps to pull customer data effectively.

### User Sentiment
- Birdeye earned a 4.7 out of 5 stars on Software Advice's FrontRunners list. It's a good fit for smaller organizations, with 60% of reviewers hailing from companies with two to 50 employees.
- Verified reviewers call it "a powerful and reliable platform that makes reputation management easier" but note it "can be a bit pricey, especially for smaller teams or businesses on a tighter budget. Some of the most useful advanced features are only available on higher-tier plans."
