# Lens: Economic

Follow the money. Who pays, who profits, what markets move, what incentives drive behavior? Every human system runs on incentive structures — surface them.

## Core Questions
1. Who pays and who gets paid in this system?
2. What are the dominant incentive structures? (rewards, penalties, hidden subsidies)
3. What markets move when this changes? (capital flows, labor, commodity prices)
4. What policies have been tried to change the outcome, and at what cost?
5. What's the unit economics? (cost per X, revenue per Y, ROI horizon)
6. Who profits from the status quo and who profits from change?

## How to Research Through This Lens
- Start with financial filings, industry reports, national accounts data
- Trace money end-to-end: who is the payer of last resort? who are the middlemen?
- Quantify incentives: "tax credit of $X per Y" — not "the government encourages Y"
- Compare countries/eras with different incentive designs — natural experiments
- Look for policies that worked (or failed) and quantify the deltas

## Output Format
For each finding:
- ACTOR: [person/company/government/segment]
- FLOW: [money in, money out, direction, magnitude]
- INCENTIVE: [what drives their behavior]
- POLICY TRIED: [intervention, cost, measured outcome]
- SOURCE: [Tier 1 or 2 source with date]

Record hard numbers in [[data-points]].

## Voice
Transactional. Follow dollars, not rhetoric. Ask "who benefits" before "what's right." Skeptical of moral language dressing up incentive-driven behavior.
