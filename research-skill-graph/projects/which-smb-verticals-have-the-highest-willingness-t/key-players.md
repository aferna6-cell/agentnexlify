# Which SMB verticals have the highest willingness to pay for AI appointment booking and why?

**Depth:** standard  |  **Model:** claude-sonnet-4-6  |  **Date:** 2026-04-18

**Platform Incumbents (Platform Absorption Threat)**
- **ServiceTitan** — dominant home services FSM, $600M+ ARR, actively shipping AI features (Titan Intelligence); most likely to absorb AI booking in home services vertical within 24–36 months
- **Jobber** — home services platform, $250M+ ARR; acquired Goodcall (AI phone booking) in 2024; direct signal of home services AI booking absorption
- **Weave** — SMB dental/medical communication platform, ~$200M ARR; already includes AI communication features; most likely AI booking absorber in dental vertical
- **NexHealth** — dental/medical booking and patient communication platform; direct AI booking incumbent in dental
- **Clio** — legal practice management, 150,000+ users; basic scheduling but AI booking layer is wide open; likely acquirer or builder for legal AI booking
- **Mindbody** — fitness/wellness booking, taken private at $1.9B; dominates the vertical and is adding AI features; high barrier for standalone entrants

**Active AI Booking Vendors (Direct Competitors / Market Validators)**
- **Slang.ai** — restaurant/hospitality voice AI booking; raised $20M; validates the category but also illustrates restaurant margin trap
- **Goodcall** — SMB voice AI booking (acquired by Jobber 2024); validated home services WTP and acquisition path
- **PolyAI** — enterprise voice AI with SMB applications; higher price point; validates voice booking market
- **Numa** — SMB AI text messaging and booking for automotive + home services; ~$40M raised; relevant competitor

**Vertical-Specific Booking Platforms (Reveal WTP Norms)**
- **OpenTable** (restaurants) — $249/month; acquired by Priceline for $2.6B; established restaurant booking SaaS norms
- **Boulevard** (salons) — $175/month for multi-location; raised $70M; validated premium salon WTP
- **Vagaro** (salons/wellness) — $30–$130/month; established mass-market salon WTP ceiling
- **StyleSeat** (independent stylists) — validated low WTP ($35/month) for solo operators
- **Zocdoc** (healthcare) — per-booking pricing + SaaS; validated healthcare booking WTP
