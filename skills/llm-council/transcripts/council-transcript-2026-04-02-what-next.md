## Chairman's Synthesis

### Where the Council Agrees
Every advisor said the same thing: stop building, start selling. The chatbot fix is the minimum condition to find out if there's a business. Keys Koffee must be contacted this week. No new features.

### Where the Council Clashes
- Psychology vs. action: First Principles names avoidance, Executor gives a plan, neither alone is sufficient
- Pace: Outsider says 1 customer deep, Expansionist says 20 pilots wide — solo operator can't do both
- Data validity: Contrarian says past learnings are suspect, Expansionist says mine them for a case study

### Blind Spots the Council Caught
1. **3 signups + 0 usage = onboarding is broken.** Keys Koffee didn't fail to see value — they failed to reach value. Fix/understand that before calling.
2. **Who is Keys Koffee?** 3 different emails — is the decision-maker the signer-upper?
3. **No success criteria.** What does week 4 look like if this is working vs. not?

### The Recommendation
Two weeks, two objectives, one rule.

**Objective 1:** Get Keys Koffee live and paying. Their widget on their website, answering real messages. This is product research that generates revenue if it works.

**Objective 2:** Identify 3 other local businesses by end of week 2. Not pitch — identify. Businesses with high inbound volume, no chat tool.

**The rule:** Nothing gets built until a paying customer asks for it. Not "implies" — asks.

On pricing: don't change $249 yet. It's a hypothesis that needs at least one rejection to test.

### The One Thing to Do First
Find out who at Keys Koffee signed up. Check the database for their name and phone. Answer: owner, manager, or curious? Then send one email today — not a pitch, one sentence about what's fixed and one question: "Would you have 20 minutes this week to try it with me watching?" If no response in 48 hours, call. Keys Koffee is a coffee shop — they have a door.

Everything else waits for that conversation.
