## Services

### Residential

**House Washing**
Soft-wash exterior cleaning for vinyl, brick, stucco, wood, and Hardie board siding. Removes mold, mildew, algae, dirt, and cobwebs using a low-pressure technique that will not damage paint or siding.

**Driveway and Sidewalk Cleaning**
High-pressure surface cleaning for concrete, pavers, asphalt, and stamped concrete. Removes oil stains, tire marks, algae, and ground-in dirt. We use a surface cleaner for even, streak-free results.

**Deck and Patio Cleaning**
Restore wood, composite, Trex, and stone surfaces. Removes green algae, grime, and weathering. Sealant application available after cleaning — ask for pricing.

**Gutter Cleaning and Brightening**
Remove debris from inside gutters and wash the exterior face to eliminate black tiger stripes and oxidation.

**Roof Cleaning**
Soft-wash only — no high pressure on roofs. Removes black streaks (Gloeocapsa magma), moss, lichen, and algae. Safe for asphalt shingles, tile, and metal roofs.

**Fence Cleaning**
Wood and vinyl fence washing. Removes green buildup, mildew, and weathering.

**Concrete Sealing**
After cleaning, we offer concrete sealing to protect driveways, patios, and pool decks from staining and weather damage.

### Commercial

**Storefront and Building Washing**
Keep your business looking professional. We clean exterior walls, sidewalks, entryways, and awnings.

**Parking Lot and Garage Cleaning**
Remove oil, gum, and grime from parking surfaces.

**Dumpster Pad Cleaning**
Sanitize and deodorize dumpster areas.

**Fleet Washing**
On-site washing for company vehicles, trucks, trailers, and heavy equipment.

**Graffiti Removal**
Specialized cleaning to remove graffiti from most exterior surfaces.

**How much does power washing cost?**
Pricing depends on the size of the area, the type of surface, and the level of buildup. A typical house wash ranges from $250 to $500 and a standard two-car driveway is usually $100 to $200. These are sample ranges — we provide a free estimate tailored to your specific job.

**What surfaces do you clean?**
We clean most exterior surfaces: vinyl, brick, stucco, and wood siding; concrete, paver, and asphalt driveways; wood and composite decks; patios; roofs; fences; gutters; and commercial buildings. We use the right method for each surface.

**Do you use chemicals? Is it safe for plants and pets?**
We use biodegradable, SDS-compliant detergents that are safe for plants, pets, and children. For house and roof washing we use a low-concentration sodium hypochlorite-based soft wash solution. We pre-wet and rinse all surrounding landscaping before and after.

**How long does it take?**
Most residential jobs take 1 to 3 hours. A house wash is typically 1 to 2 hours; a driveway takes about 30 to 60 minutes. Larger jobs or heavy staining take longer. We give you a time estimate with your quote.

**Do I need to be home?**
No — as long as we have access to the areas being cleaned and an outdoor water spigot. We coordinate access beforehand and send before-and-after photos when the job is complete.

**What is the difference between pressure washing and soft washing?**
Pressure washing uses high-pressure water — effective for hard surfaces like concrete and brick. ## Objection Handlers

**"I can just rent a pressure washer myself."**
A pressure washer rental is $50–$100 per day, plus your time, plus the risk of surface damage from wrong pressure or technique. Professional equipment is 3–5x more powerful and our team knows exactly which method each surface needs. Most customers find the cost difference is smaller than they expect once they factor in time and risk.

**"Your prices seem high."**
We price based on the actual size and condition of the job — our estimate is free and there is no obligation. Many customers are surprised when the quote comes in lower than expected once we see the property. Would you like to request a free estimate?

**"I need to think about it / I am just getting prices."**
Absolutely — no pressure. Our estimates are free and valid for 30 days (DEMO-SAMPLE). If you want to compare, just request ours and we will have it to you same day. No commitment required.

**"Do you guarantee your work?"**
Yes. If you are not satisfied with any part of the job, we will return and re-clean the area at no additional charge. We stand behind our work.

**"Can you do [interior cleaning / painting / landscaping]?"**
That is outside our area of expertise — we focus on exterior pressure and soft washing. I would suggest searching for a specialist in that area. Is there anything we can help with on the exterior?
