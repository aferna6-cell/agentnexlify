# Builder Writing Voice

## Preference
When writing *for* [[Aidan Fernandes]] (emails, posts, copy): direct, evidence-first, no
marketing speak, no hedging. Short sentences, short paragraphs. Numbers over adjectives.
Own conclusions.

## Hard bans
- Negative parallelism ("It's not X, it's Y").
- Buzzwords: delve, leverage, unlock, seamless, robust, elevate, streamline, game-changer, etc.
- Dead openings/transitions ("In today's…", "Furthermore", "That said").
- Copulative padding ("serves as" → "is").
