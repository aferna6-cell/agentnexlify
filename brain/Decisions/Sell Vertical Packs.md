# Decision: Sell Vertical Packs, Not a Generic Chatbot

## Decision
Position and sell AgentNexLiFy as **vertical-specific packs on one shared engine**. "Do not
sell AI automation as the main noun."

## Rationale
Vertical framing converts better with owner-operated trades and reinforces the
[[Vertical Knowledge-Base Moat]]; generic-chatbot framing competes on a commodity axis.

## Alternatives Considered
- Sell a horizontal "AI chatbot/automation" product (rejected — commoditized, weak moat).

## Consequences
- Build order: Contractors/Home Services → Salons/Spas → Dental/Local Health → Auto Detailers.
- Drives the [[Vertical Packs]] project and exposes the depth gap (G8).
