# Vertical Knowledge-Base Moat

## Definition
AgentNexLiFy's stated competitive moat: a **per-tenant vertical knowledge base** drives replies,
not a generic LLM. Combined with selling [[Vertical Packs]], this is the differentiation against
horizontal competitors like [[GoHighLevel]].

## Why it matters
- Generic AI replies are commoditized; tenant- + vertical-specific knowledge is defensible.
- The internal [[Knowledge Base Wiki]] is the team's own application of the same pattern.
