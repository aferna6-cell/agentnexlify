# Proactive AI Opportunities Job

## What
Today the AI is fully **reactive**. Build a nightly per-tenant "opportunities" job that surfaces
proactive actions — cold-lead nudges and unpaid-invoice reminders. Gap **G7**.

## Why it's an open loop
Proactivity is what makes the product feel like an employee, not a tool; differentiates from
reactive chatbots.
