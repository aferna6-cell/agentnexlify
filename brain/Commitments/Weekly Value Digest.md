# Weekly Value Digest

## What
Send every paying tenant a **dollar-framed weekly summary** email (leads captured, bookings,
value delivered). Gap **G2** — the highest open product-gap priority. ~half a day of work.

## Why it's an open loop
Small businesses need to *see* the value to stay; the digest is the cheapest retention lever.
