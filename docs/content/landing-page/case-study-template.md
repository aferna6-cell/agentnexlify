## Notes for filling this in:

- **Get specific numbers.** "3x more leads" is good. "We went from 10 to 30 leads per month" is better.

- **Include before/after.** The contrast makes the value obvious.

- **Photos if possible.** A photo of the business or the owner adds credibility.
