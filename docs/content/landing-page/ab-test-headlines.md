# Landing Page A/B Test — 3 Hero Headlines

Test these variants to find which messaging resonates best with small business owners.

---

## Variant A: The Problem

**Headline:** You're Losing Leads While You Sleep

**Subheadline:** Every night, potential customers visit your website, have questions, and leave because nobody's there. AgentNexLiFy's AI assistant answers instantly — 24/7 — so you never miss another lead.

**CTA:** Start Capturing Leads Free

**Why this might win:** Leads with fear of loss. "While you sleep" is visceral and true for every business owner who's ever checked their phone in the morning.

---

## Variant B: The Outcome

**Headline:** Your Website, Working as Hard as You Do

**Subheadline:** An AI assistant that answers questions, captures contact info, and books appointments — right on your website. Set it up in 10 minutes. No coding. No hiring.

**CTA:** Get Your Free AI Assistant

**Why this might win:** Leads with aspiration. "Working as hard as you do" resonates with business owners who feel overworked. The "10 minutes, no coding" removes the friction objection.

---

## Variant C: The Social Proof

**Headline:** The AI Assistant That Turns Website Visitors Into Customers

**Subheadline:** Small businesses using AI chat assistants capture 5x more leads from the same website traffic. AgentNexLiFy sets up in minutes and starts working immediately.

**CTA:** See It In Action

**Why this might win:** Leads with data. The "5x" stat is compelling and specific. "See It In Action" is lower commitment than "Sign Up" — good for skeptics.

---

## Testing Plan

1. Run all 3 variants for 2 weeks minimum
2. Measure: signup conversion rate, time on page, bounce rate
3. Need 100+ signups per variant for statistical significance
4. Winner becomes the permanent headline; losers inform future messaging

## Supporting Elements (Same Across All Variants)

**Trust signals:**
- "Used by 500+ local businesses" (when we hit that milestone)
- "Set up in under 10 minutes"
- "No credit card required"
- "Cancel anytime"

**Feature bullets (below the fold):**
- AI that knows your business (FAQ + website scanning)
- Appointment booking built in
- Lead capture on autopilot
- Works 24/7, even holidays
- Multilingual (responds in the visitor's language)

**Social proof section:**
- Testimonial from a real customer (when available)
- "Businesses using AgentNexLiFy capture 3-5x more leads from the same website traffic"
