# Setting Up Appointment Booking

Let your chat widget book appointments for you. Visitors pick a time, you get a confirmation, and it shows up on your calendar.

## Step 1: Set Your Availability

Go to **Availability** in the sidebar:

1. **Timezone** — Set your business timezone. All appointments use this timezone.
2. **Weekly hours** — Toggle each day on/off and set start/end times. Example: Monday-Friday 9 AM - 5 PM.
3. **Slot duration** — How long each appointment is (30 min, 60 min, etc.).

## Step 2: Enable Booking in the Widget

Go to **Widget** in the sidebar:

1. Find the **Booking** section
2. Toggle **Booking Enabled** on
3. Save

Now when visitors chat with your AI, it can offer to book appointments and show available time slots.

## Step 3: Connect Google Calendar (Optional)

Go to **Integrations** in the sidebar:

1. Click **Connect Google Calendar**
2. Sign in with your Google account
3. Grant permission to manage events

Once connected, every booked appointment automatically appears on your Google Calendar. Changes sync both ways.

## How Booking Works

1. A visitor chats with your AI assistant
2. The AI detects they want to schedule something (repair, consultation, etc.)
3. The AI offers available time slots based on your business hours
4. The visitor picks a time
5. The appointment is created and confirmation is sent via email
6. You see it on your Calendar page (and Google Calendar if connected)

## Managing Appointments

Click **Calendar** in the sidebar to see all your appointments:

- **Month view** — See your full schedule at a glance
- **Click any appointment** to see details, edit, or cancel
- **Status tracking** — Appointments are "Booked" by default. Update to "Completed" or "Cancelled"

## Recurring Appointments

For repeat customers (cleaning, maintenance, etc.):

1. Click an existing appointment
2. Click **Make Recurring**
3. Choose: Weekly, Biweekly, or Monthly
4. Set an end date
5. The system creates all future appointments automatically

## Automatic Reminders

Appointment reminders are sent automatically:

- **24 hours before** — Email + SMS reminder
- **1 hour before** — Email + SMS final reminder

This reduces no-shows by up to 40%. No setup needed — it works automatically once booking is enabled.

## Tips

- **Keep hours up to date.** If you're closed for a holiday, update your availability so the AI doesn't offer those slots.
- **Use buffer time.** If you need 15 minutes between appointments, account for it in your slot duration.
- **Check your Calendar daily.** New bookings appear immediately.
- **Connect Google Calendar.** It prevents double-booking if you also take appointments through other channels.
