# How to Configure Your AI Assistant

Your AgentNexLiFy AI assistant learns about your business and talks to customers on your behalf. The more you teach it, the better it gets. Here's how to set it up so it sounds like you.

## Step 1: Add Your Business Information

Go to **Settings** in the sidebar. Fill in:

- **Business Name** — This is how the AI introduces itself ("Hi! Welcome to [Your Business]")
- **Business Type** — Helps the AI understand your industry (plumber, restaurant, salon, etc.)
- **Website URL** — The AI will scan your website and learn about your services automatically

Once you save your website URL, click **Scan Website**. The AI reads your site and immediately knows your services, hours, and offerings — no manual entry needed.

## Step 2: Add FAQs

Go to **FAQ Manager** in the sidebar. Add the questions your customers ask most:

- "What are your hours?"
- "How much does [service] cost?"
- "Do you serve [area]?"
- "What's your cancellation policy?"

For each question, write the answer exactly how you'd say it. The AI uses these word-for-word when relevant, so make them sound natural.

**Tip:** Start with 5-10 FAQs. You can always add more later when you see what customers are asking in the Conversations tab.

## Step 3: Set Up Business Hours

Go to **Availability** in the sidebar. Set your weekly hours and timezone. The AI uses this to:

- Tell visitors when you're open or closed
- Show available appointment slots (if booking is enabled)
- Display an offline contact form outside business hours

## Step 4: Customize the Widget

Go to **Widget** in the sidebar to change:

- **Bot Name** — What your assistant calls itself
- **Greeting Message** — The first thing visitors see when the chat opens
- **Primary Color** — Match your brand
- **Position** — Bottom-right or bottom-left of the page
- **Online/Offline Toggle** — Turn the chat on or off manually

## Step 5: Train with Feedback

After the AI starts chatting with visitors, you can improve its responses:

1. Go to **Conversations** to read what the AI said
2. On any AI message, click the **thumbs down** button if the response wasn't good
3. Type what the AI *should* have said instead
4. The AI learns from your corrections — it won't make the same mistake twice

You can review all feedback in **Settings > AI Feedback**.

## Step 6: Set Up Chat Flows (Optional)

For more control over the conversation, go to **Chat Flows** in the sidebar:

1. Click **From Template** to start with a preset flow for your business type
2. Or click **+ New Flow** to build one from scratch
3. Add nodes: Greeting, Questions, Conditions (if/else), Actions (show booking form, show menu), and Handoffs (transfer to your team)
4. Connect them by dragging between node handles
5. Click **Activate** to make the flow live

The AI follows your flow first, then falls back to its general knowledge for anything not covered.

## Step 7: Enable Appointment Booking

If you want the AI to book appointments:

1. Go to **Availability** and set your hours
2. Go to **Widget** and toggle **Booking Enabled** on
3. Go to **Integrations** and connect Google Calendar (optional — syncs appointments to your calendar)

The AI will offer to book time slots during conversations and send confirmation emails.

## Tips for Best Results

- **Be specific in FAQs.** "Starting at $150 for a basic service" is better than "Contact us for pricing."
- **Update regularly.** Add new FAQs when you notice the AI doesn't know something.
- **Use the thumbs down.** 5 corrections teach the AI more than 50 FAQs.
- **Check Conversations weekly.** See what customers are asking and how the AI handles it.
- **Add your team.** Go to **Team** to invite team members who can reply to conversations directly.

## Need Help?

If the AI isn't responding the way you want, check these common fixes:

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| AI gives wrong info | Add or update the relevant FAQ |
| AI sounds too generic | Scan your website or add more specific FAQs |
| AI won't book appointments | Check that booking is enabled in Widget settings and hours are set in Availability |
| AI stops responding | Check the Widget page — it might be toggled offline |
| Visitors can't see the chat | Make sure the embed code is on your website ([see guide](./how-to-embed-the-widget.md)) |
