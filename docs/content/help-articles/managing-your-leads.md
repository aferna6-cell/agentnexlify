# Managing Your Leads

Every visitor who shares their name, email, or phone through your chat widget becomes a lead. Here's how to manage them effectively.

## The Leads Page

Click **Leads** in the sidebar. You'll see your leads in two views:

- **Board view** — Leads organized by status (New, Contacted, Appointment Booked, Closed, Lost). Drag to move between stages.
- **List view** — All leads in a sortable table. Click column headers to sort by score, name, or date.

## Lead Statuses

| Status | What it means |
|--------|---------------|
| **New** | Just came in. Follow up ASAP. |
| **Contacted** | You've reached out (auto-set when you send an email from the dashboard). |
| **Appointment Booked** | They've scheduled a meeting. |
| **Closed** | They became a customer. |
| **Lost** | They're no longer interested. |

## Following Up on Leads

Click any lead to open their detail panel. From there you can:

1. **Send an email** — Click "Send Email", write your message, and hit send. The lead's status automatically changes to "Contacted."
2. **View their conversation** — See what they discussed with your AI assistant.
3. **Update their info** — Edit name, email, phone, or status.
4. **Assign to a team member** — Use the assignment dropdown to delegate follow-up.
5. **View AI suggestions** — If the AI found updated info from a later conversation, you'll see a purple suggestion banner to approve or dismiss.

## Lead Score

Every lead gets an AI-generated score from 0-100:

- **70+** (Hot) — High engagement, provided detailed info, expressed urgency
- **40-69** (Warm) — Moderate interest, some info shared
- **Under 40** (Cold) — Minimal engagement, browsing only

Sort by lead score to prioritize your follow-ups. The hottest leads should hear from you first.

## Filtering and Search

- **Search** — Type a name, email, or phone to find specific leads
- **Stage filter** — Show only leads in a specific stage
- **Assignee filter** — Show leads assigned to a specific team member (or unassigned)

## Importing Leads

Already have leads in a spreadsheet? Click **Import CSV** and upload a .csv file with columns for name, email, and phone. The system deduplicates by email automatically.

## Finding Duplicates

Click **Find Duplicates** to scan for leads with matching email or phone numbers. Review matches and merge them — the system keeps the most complete record.

## Exporting

Need your leads in a spreadsheet? Use the export button to download as CSV.

## Tips

- **Follow up within 5 minutes.** Leads that hear back quickly are 21x more likely to convert.
- **Check daily.** Sort by "New" leads and work through them.
- **Use automations.** Set up a follow-up sequence in the Automations tab so leads get an email immediately, even if you're busy.
- **Assign leads.** If you have a team, assign leads to the right person. Nobody's lead should sit unassigned.
