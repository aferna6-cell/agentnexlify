# Using Documents & E-Signatures

Send contracts, proposals, and agreements to your clients for digital signature — right from your dashboard.

## Creating a Document

1. Go to **Documents** in your sidebar
2. Click **Create Document**
3. Fill in:
   - **Title**: Name your document (e.g., "Service Agreement - Smith")
   - **Content**: Write or paste your document HTML
   - **Signer Info**: Name, email, and/or phone of the person who needs to sign
   - **Link to Lead**: Optionally connect the document to an existing lead
   - **Expiry**: Set how many days the signing link stays active (optional)

## Using Templates

Save time by creating reusable templates:

1. Create a document with your standard contract text
2. Click **Save as Template** and give it a name
3. Next time, click **From Template** when creating a new document
4. The template content is pre-filled — just update the signer info

**Tip**: Use `{{variable_name}}` placeholders in templates. When creating from a template, you'll be prompted to fill in the values.

## Sending for Signature

1. Open a document from your list
2. Click **Send**
3. Choose how to send: **Email**, **SMS**, or **Both**
4. The recipient gets a unique signing link

The document status updates automatically:
- **Draft** → **Sent** → **Viewed** → **Signed**

## Tracking Signatures

Your documents list shows the current status of every document:
- Filter by status (draft, sent, viewed, signed, expired)
- See when it was sent, viewed, and signed
- Copy the signing link to share manually

## After Signing

When a document is signed:
- The signature and timestamp are recorded
- The signer's IP address is logged for verification
- An activity log entry is created on the linked lead
- You can view the signed document from the Documents page

## Tips

- **Set expiry dates** for time-sensitive offers or contracts
- **Link to leads** so you can track which clients have signed
- **Use email + SMS** for important documents to maximize response rate
- Documents can only be deleted while in **draft** status
