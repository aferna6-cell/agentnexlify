# Setting Up Service Types for Booking

Define the services you offer so clients can pick the right one when booking — and each service gets the right time slot.

## Why Service Types Matter

Without service types, every appointment uses the same default duration (usually 30 minutes). But a quick haircut needs 30 minutes while a full color treatment needs 2 hours. Service types let you:

- **Set different durations** per service (15 min to 8 hours)
- **Show a service menu** in your booking widget
- **Include pricing** so clients know what to expect
- **Track which services are most popular**

## Setting Up Your Services

1. Go to **Settings** > **Availability** (or the Appointments section)
2. Find the **Service Types** section
3. Click **Add Service Type**
4. Fill in:
   - **Name**: What the service is called (e.g., "Haircut", "Consultation", "Repair")
   - **Duration**: How long it takes in minutes
   - **Description**: A brief description (optional, shown to clients)
   - **Price**: What you charge (optional, shown to clients)
5. Click **Save**

## How Clients See It

When a client books through your chat widget:
1. They pick a date
2. They see available time slots (filtered by the service's duration)
3. They select their service type
4. They fill in their contact info
5. The appointment is created with the right duration

## Managing Services

- **Deactivate** a service instead of deleting it (keeps historical data)
- **Reorder** services to control the display order
- Services with prices show the price in the booking flow

## Tips

- Start with your 3-5 most popular services
- Include a "Consultation" or "Free Estimate" option at 15-30 minutes
- Update prices seasonally without creating new services
- Use descriptions to set expectations ("includes wash and style")
