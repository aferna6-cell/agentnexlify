# Understanding Your Analytics Dashboard

Your Analytics page shows how your AI assistant is performing. Use these numbers to understand your leads, conversations, and business trends.

## Getting There

Click **Analytics** in the sidebar. You'll see a time period selector at the top — choose 7 days, 30 days, or 90 days.

## The Metrics

### Conversations

- **Total Conversations** — How many chat sessions happened in the selected period
- **Trend** — Up or down compared to the previous period (green = growing, red = declining)

More conversations means more people are engaging with your website. If this number is low, make sure the widget is visible and the greeting message is inviting.

### Leads Captured

- **Total Leads** — How many visitors shared their contact info (name, email, or phone)
- **Conversion Rate** — What percentage of conversations turned into leads

A healthy conversion rate is 15-30%. If yours is low, the AI might need better FAQs or a more engaging greeting.

### Appointments Booked

- **Total Appointments** — How many visitors booked time with you through the chat
- **Booking Rate** — Appointments as a percentage of conversations

If booking is enabled but this number is zero, check that your availability hours are set correctly.

### Response Times

- **Average Response Time** — How fast the AI responds to the first message in a conversation
- **Response Time Trend** — Chart showing response times over the period

The AI typically responds in under 3 seconds. If you see spikes, there may have been a temporary service issue.

### Lead Conversion Funnel

This chart shows the journey from visitor to customer:

1. **Conversations Started** — Everyone who chatted
2. **Info Collected** — Visitors who shared contact details
3. **Appointments Booked** — Visitors who scheduled a meeting
4. **Closed Won** — Leads you marked as customers

Each step shows how many people moved forward and where they dropped off. Focus on the biggest drop-off point to improve your results.

### Busiest Hours Heat Map

A grid showing which hours of which days get the most conversations. Use this to:

- **Staff up** during peak hours (if you have team members handling chats)
- **Optimize offline hours** — If you get lots of chats at 9 PM but you close at 6 PM, the AI is capturing leads you'd otherwise miss
- **Schedule promotions** — Post on social media right before your busiest chat hours

### Missed Opportunities

This section flags conversations where you might have lost a potential customer:

- **No lead captured** — The visitor chatted but didn't share contact info. Consider adding a more natural lead capture prompt.
- **Long response time** — The visitor waited too long for a response (this is rare with AI but can happen during outages).
- **Dropped conversation** — The visitor left mid-conversation. Check if the AI's responses were helpful.

### Tag Distribution

If you have AI conversation tags enabled, this chart shows how conversations break down by category (New Lead, Pricing Question, Complaint, etc.). Use this to understand what your customers ask about most.

## What Good Looks Like

| Metric | Good | Needs Work |
|--------|------|------------|
| Conversations/week | 20+ | Under 10 |
| Lead conversion rate | 20%+ | Under 10% |
| Avg response time | Under 5s | Over 15s |
| Missed opportunities | Under 20% | Over 40% |

## Tips

- **Check weekly, not daily.** Day-to-day numbers fluctuate. Weekly trends tell the real story.
- **Compare periods.** Use the time selector to compare this month vs. last month.
- **Act on missed opportunities.** Each one is a real person who almost became your customer.
- **Share with your team.** If you have team members, review analytics together to decide where to improve.
