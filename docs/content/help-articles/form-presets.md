# Using Form Presets

Create professional intake forms in one click with industry-specific templates.

## Available Presets

| Preset | Best For | Fields |
|--------|----------|--------|
| **New Patient Health History** | Dental, Medical | 13 fields: name, DOB, insurance, allergies, medications, conditions, consent |
| **Patient Registration** | Medical, Healthcare | 11 fields: name, DOB, insurance, medical history, reason for visit |
| **Request a Free Estimate** | Contractors, Home Services | 8 fields: name, address, service type, description, timeline, photos |
| **New Client Intake** | Law Firms | 10 fields: name, case type, description, opposing party, deadlines |
| **Fitness Liability Waiver** | Gyms, Studios | 10 fields: name, emergency contact, health conditions, fitness goals, waiver |

## Creating a Form from a Preset

1. Go to **Forms** in your sidebar
2. Click **Create Form**
3. Look for the **Use Preset** dropdown
4. Select your industry template
5. The form is created instantly with all fields pre-configured
6. Customize any fields, add your own, or adjust the success message

## After Creating

Your form gets a unique public URL and embed code:
- **Share the link** directly with clients via email or text
- **Embed on your website** using the HTML snippet
- **Track submissions** on the Forms page with status and lead linking

## Customizing Presets

After creating from a preset, you can:
- Add or remove fields
- Change field labels and placeholders
- Make fields required or optional
- Update the success message
- Add your brand colors

The preset gives you a head start — customize it to match your exact needs.

## Tips

- **Dental offices**: Use the health history preset before first appointments
- **Contractors**: Send the estimate request form to website visitors
- **Law firms**: Embed the intake form on your website's "Contact Us" page
- **Gyms**: Have new members complete the waiver on a tablet at the front desk
