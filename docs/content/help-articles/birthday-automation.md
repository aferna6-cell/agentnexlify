# How to Set Up Birthday Automation

Automatically send birthday greetings to your clients — a personal touch that builds loyalty and drives repeat visits.

## How It Works

AgentNexLiFy checks daily for leads with today's birthday and sends a personalized "Happy Birthday" email. Each lead only gets one birthday greeting per year.

## Setting Up Birthday Greetings

### Step 1: Add Date of Birth to Your Leads

1. Open a lead in the **Leads** page
2. Click on the lead to open the detail drawer
3. In the **Contact Info** section, find the **Date of Birth** field
4. Enter their birthday and click **Save**

**Tip:** You can also collect date of birth through:
- A form preset (dental intake, medical intake) — the DOB field is included
- Chat conversations — the AI will naturally ask during patient intake
- CSV import — include a `date_of_birth` column in YYYY-MM-DD format

### Step 2: That's It!

Birthday automation runs automatically for all paid plans. No additional configuration needed. When a lead's birthday arrives:

1. They receive a personalized email saying "Happy Birthday" from your business
2. The greeting is logged in the activity feed
3. Each lead gets exactly one greeting per year (no duplicates)

## What the Birthday Email Looks Like

The email includes:
- Personalized greeting with the lead's name
- Your business name
- A warm message encouraging them to visit again
- Professional formatting that matches your brand

## Requirements

- Lead must have a **date of birth** saved
- Lead must have an **email address**
- Your business must be on a **paid plan** (Growth, Professional, or Enterprise)
- The lead must NOT be unsubscribed

## FAQ

**Can I customize the birthday message?**
Not yet — the default message works well for most businesses. Custom birthday templates are on the roadmap.

**When does the email send?**
Birthday emails are checked multiple times daily. Your lead will receive their greeting on their birthday.

**Does it work for SMS too?**
Currently email only. SMS birthday greetings are planned for a future update.

**What if I add a lead's birthday after it's already passed this year?**
They'll receive a greeting on their next birthday.
