# Managing Your Sales Pipeline

Track every lead's journey from first contact to closed deal with a visual Kanban board.

## What is the Pipeline?

The pipeline shows all your leads organized by stage — from new inquiry to closed deal. Drag leads between columns to update their progress. See your total deal value at a glance.

## Getting Started

When you first open the **Pipeline** page, stages are automatically created based on your business type:

| Business Type | Default Stages |
|--------------|----------------|
| Real Estate | Lead → Contacted → Showing → Offer → Under Contract → Closed |
| Contractor | Lead → Estimate Sent → Approved → In Progress → Completed |
| Dental | New Patient → Consulted → Treatment Planned → In Treatment → Completed |
| Legal | Inquiry → Consultation → Retained → Active Case → Resolved |
| Salon | New Client → Consulted → Booked → Regular Client |
| Fitness | Trial → Consulted → Member → At Risk → Churned |
| Other | Lead → Contacted → Qualified → Proposal Sent → Won |

## Moving Leads Through the Pipeline

1. Find the lead card in its current column
2. Click the lead to see details
3. Use the **Move to** dropdown to change their stage
4. The lead moves to the new column

## Pipeline Analytics

At the top of the Pipeline page, you'll see:
- **Total Pipeline Value** — sum of all deal values across stages
- **Conversion Rate** — percentage of leads that reach the "Won" stage
- **Average Days to Close** — how long deals typically take

## Tips

- **Add deal values** to leads so you can track revenue in the pipeline
- **Customize stages** — add, rename, or reorder stages in Settings
- **Focus on bottlenecks** — if leads pile up in one stage, that's where to improve
- **Review weekly** — move stale leads to "Lost" to keep your pipeline clean
