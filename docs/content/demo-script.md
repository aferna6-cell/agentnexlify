# AgentNexLiFy — Client Demo Script

**Duration:** 15-20 minutes
**Audience:** Small business owner considering the platform

---

## Opening (1 min)

"Let me show you how AgentNexLiFy works. By the end of this demo, you'll see how your business can capture more leads, book more appointments, and follow up automatically — without hiring extra staff."

---

## 1. Signup & AI Configuration (2 min)

**Show:** Signup flow at app.agentnexlify.com

"When you sign up, you tell us your business name, type, phone, and website. That's it."

**Key moments:**
- Business type dropdown → "Watch what happens when I select [their industry]"
- Auto-generated FAQs appear → "The AI already knows your industry. These FAQ answers train your chat assistant immediately."
- Website crawl → "If you have a website, we scan it and teach the AI about YOUR business specifically."

**Wow factor:** "Within 60 seconds of signing up, your AI assistant already knows how to answer questions about your business."

---

## 2. The Widget — Your 24/7 Sales Agent (3 min)

**Show:** Widget preview on WidgetPage

"This chat widget goes on your website. It works 24/7, even when you're closed."

**Demo the chat:**
- Visitor asks "What services do you offer?" → AI answers from FAQs
- Visitor asks about pricing → AI responds naturally
- Visitor wants to book → AI shows the booking calendar

**Key moments:**
- "The AI captures their name, email, and phone naturally — no forms to fill out"
- "If someone mentions an emergency, their lead score automatically goes to 'Hot'"
- Show the booking flow: pick date → pick time → enter details → confirmed

**Wow factor:** "This is like having a receptionist who never sleeps, never takes a break, and never loses a lead."

---

## 3. Lead Management (2 min)

**Show:** LeadsPage

"Every person who chats becomes a lead. Watch how the system scores them."

**Key moments:**
- AI conversation summary under each lead name
- Lead score with Hot/Warm/Cold temperature
- Tags auto-extracted from conversations
- Click lead → detail drawer with contact info, insurance fields, notes

**For dental/medical:** Show insurance carrier, member ID, group number fields
**For contractors:** Show areas of interest, budget, timeline

---

## 4. Sales Pipeline (2 min)

**Show:** PipelinePage

"Your pipeline is customized for your industry."

**Key moments:**
- Industry-specific stages (show their business type's stages)
- Drag leads between columns
- Deal values and pipeline analytics

**Wow factor:** "Most CRMs make you set this up manually. We do it automatically based on your business type."

---

## 5. Appointment Booking (2 min)

**Show:** Calendar + Availability settings

"Your clients book directly through the chat widget. No phone tag."

**Key moments:**
- Service types with different durations → "A cleaning takes 30 minutes, a root canal takes 90"
- Business hours configuration
- Automatic reminders: 24h + 1h before appointment
- For dental: "The 24-hour reminder tells them to bring their insurance card"

---

## 6. Automation (3 min)

**Show:** Automations page + explain the pipeline

"Here's where it gets powerful. Everything happens automatically."

**Walk through the automation pipeline:**
1. "Someone books an appointment → they get a reminder with what to bring"
2. "They come in → appointment marked complete"
3. "2 hours later → they get aftercare instructions" (show dental-specific example)
4. "24 hours later → they get a rebook suggestion: 'Schedule your next cleaning in 6 months'"
5. "Then → they get a review request"
6. "On their birthday → they get a birthday greeting"

**Wow factor:** "You set this up once. Then it runs forever. Every client gets perfect follow-up."

---

## 7. Invoicing & Payments (2 min)

**Show:** InvoicesPage

"Send professional invoices with one click. Clients pay via Stripe."

**Key moments:**
- Create invoice with saved line items → "Your most common services are saved"
- Deposit required → "Collect a deposit upfront for big jobs"
- Send via email + SMS → client gets a payment link
- Partial payments → track progress with a visual bar

---

## 8. Documents & E-Signatures (1 min)

**Show:** DocumentsPage

"Send contracts for signature. No more printing and scanning."

**Key moments:**
- Create from template → fill in client details
- Send for signature → client signs on their phone
- Status tracking: sent → viewed → signed

---

## 9. Analytics (1 min)

**Show:** AnalyticsPage

"See exactly what's working."

**Key moments:**
- Lead source breakdown → "Know which channel brings the most leads"
- Response time trends
- Conversion funnel

---

## Closing (1 min)

"So to recap: AgentNexLiFy gives you an AI receptionist that works 24/7, captures every lead, books appointments, sends reminders with what to bring, follows up automatically with aftercare and rebook suggestions, sends invoices, gets contracts signed, and requests reviews — all without you lifting a finger."

"Plans start at $249/month. Want to try it free for 14 days?"

---

## Tips for the Demo

- **Personalize:** Use the prospect's actual business type when showing features
- **Show, don't tell:** Open the actual dashboard and click through
- **Focus on time saved:** "How many hours do you spend on [task] each week?"
- **Industry examples:** Reference the simulation docs for industry-specific talking points
- **End with ROI:** "If this system captures just 2 extra leads per month, it pays for itself"
