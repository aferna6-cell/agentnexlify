# AgentNexLiFy Content Flywheel Strategy

**Goal:** Become the obvious choice for small business owners who are tired of missing leads.
**Audience:** US small business owners with 1-20 employees (33M businesses).
**Tone:** Direct, relatable, no jargon. We are not a CRM. We are relief.

---

## Content Pillars

### 1. Relatable Pain (60% of content)
Show the daily frustration of running a small business. Missed calls, voicemails, "check our website" replies.
- "POV: You're a plumber who missed 3 calls today"
- "Every missed chat is a missed customer"
- "You didn't start your business to answer emails at 11pm"

### 2. Magic Demo (25% of content)
15-30 second clips of the bot actually working. Screen recordings > polished ads.
- Bot booking an appointment in real time
- Bot sending a follow-up email while owner is on vacation
- Side-by-side: old voicemail vs. instant AI response

### 3. Founder Story / Behind the Scenes (10% of content)
Why we built this. The real human behind the product.
- "I built this because my dad's auto shop was losing leads to voicemail"
- Day-in-the-life building AgentNexLiFy
- Customer win stories (even small ones)

### 4. Education / Myth-Busting (5% of content)
- "A CRM won't fix your lead problem. Here's why."
- "The 5-minute rule: why speed beats everything"
- "You don't need more software. You need less work."

---

## Platform Strategy

### TikTok + Instagram Reels (Priority #1)
**Why:** Your buyers scroll here after work. They are not on LinkedIn reading about martech.
**Format:** 15-30 seconds, vertical video, captions required (most watch muted).
**Posting frequency:** 5x per week minimum to train the algorithm.
**Hook formulas:**
- "If you own a [business type], stop what you're doing..."
- "This one thing cost me $2,000 last month..."
- "I wish I knew this before I hired my first employee"
- "Your competitor is already doing this"

### Facebook
**Why:** Older SMB owners (35-55) live here. Local business groups are gold.
**Format:** Mixed — short videos, carousels, text posts.
**Strategy:** Join 10 local business groups. Be helpful first. Post your own content 2-3x/week.
**Never:** Drop a link and run. Answer questions, ask questions, be a real person.

### YouTube Shorts
**Why:** Google owns it. Shorts show up in search.
**Format:** Same videos as TikTok, re-uploaded.
**Strategy:** 3x per week. Title every video with a question someone would search.

### LinkedIn
**Why:** Not for your buyers, but for partners, agencies, and press.
**Format:** Longer text posts, founder updates.
**Strategy:** 1x per week. Position AgentNexLiFy as a category leader.

## The "No-Brainer" CTA Stack

Every post should make the next step obvious:

1. **Top of funnel:** "Link in bio to try free for 14 days"
2. **Mid funnel:** "Comment 'LEADS' and I'll send you our free checklist"
3. **Bottom funnel:** "DM me 'DEMO' and I'll show you your bot live"

**Bio link:** Always point to `/signup` or `free-chatbot.html`. Never Calendly.

---

## Content Production Tips

- **Batch film:** Spend 1 hour on Sunday filming 5-7 videos. Edit them Monday morning.
- **Use your phone:** Authenticity beats production value for SMB owners.
- **Captions are mandatory:** 80% watch without sound.
- **Post at 6-8 PM local time:** Business owners scroll after dinner.
- **Reply to every comment in the first 30 minutes:** This signals the algorithm to push your video.
