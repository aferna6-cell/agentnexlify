# Sales Demo Script

Use this for a 12-15 minute small-business demo. The goal is to show how
AgentNexLiFy captures leads, answers common questions, and gives the owner a
simple way to follow up.

## Positioning

AgentNexLiFy is an AI front desk for service businesses. It adds a chat widget
to the website, answers routine questions, captures qualified leads, and routes
follow-up work into the dashboard.

## Audience

- Local service businesses with missed calls or slow website response times.
- Owners who want more booked conversations without hiring another admin.
- Teams that already have a website but no reliable lead capture workflow.

## Talk Track

1. Open with the problem:
   "Most visitors leave without calling. AgentNexLiFy gives them a fast answer
   and captures the lead while intent is fresh."

2. Show the website widget:
   "This is the only thing the customer sees: a small chat bubble that matches
   the brand and starts the conversation."

3. Send a realistic visitor message:
   "Ask a service question, pricing-range question, or appointment question."

4. Show lead capture:
   "The assistant gathers the useful details instead of just saying someone
   will get back to them."

5. Show the dashboard:
   "The owner sees the conversation, contact details, and next action."

6. Show setup path:
   "Installation is a single script tag. Most customers can add it in five
   minutes, and the onboarding wizard helps fill business details from the site."

## Demo Flow

1. Landing or customer website with widget installed.
2. Widget conversation:
   - "Do you offer same-day appointments?"
   - "I need help this week. What information do you need?"
   - Provide name, phone, and service need.
3. Dashboard:
   - Open conversations/leads.
   - Show captured details.
   - Show follow-up status or action items.
4. Setup:
   - Open onboarding wizard.
   - Show website auto-fill.
   - Show widget customization.

## Objection Handling

| Objection | Response |
| --- | --- |
| "I already have a contact form." | "Forms wait for the customer to do all the work. This answers questions and captures context while they are engaged." |
| "Will it say the wrong thing?" | "You control the business facts, FAQs, and escalation rules. We also keep human review for sensitive workflows." |
| "Will setup be hard?" | "The widget is one script tag, and we provide platform-specific install steps for WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, Shopify, and HTML sites." |
| "I do not want another inbox." | "The dashboard is meant to show only useful conversations, lead details, and next actions." |

## Close

"The easiest pilot is one website, one service area, and one clear goal: capture
more qualified inquiries this month. If the widget does not create useful leads,
we have learned quickly. If it does, we expand the workflows."
