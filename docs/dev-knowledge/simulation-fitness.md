# Customer Simulation: Fitness Studio / Gym
Date: 2026-03-21

## Persona
Dave Kim, owns a boutique fitness studio. 2 trainers, 15 group classes/week, 200 members. Uses Instagram for marketing. Pain points: trial-to-membership conversion, class no-shows, member retention after 3 months.

## Journey (Using Industry-Content Skill Checklist)

### 1. FAQs ✓ (5 FAQs)
Memberships, personal training, free trial, hours, group classes. Good coverage.

### 2. Pipeline Preset ✗ Missing
No fitness-specific pipeline. Would need: Trial → Consulted → Member → At Risk → Churned.

No fitness waiver/liability form. Gyms require a liability waiver before first workout.

### 4. Reminders ✓
"Comfortable workout clothes, Water bottle, Towel" — good.

### 5. Aftercare ✓
"Great session! Stay hydrated, stretch, and rest as needed." — good.

### 6. Rebook ✓ (30 days)
Rebook suggestion after 30 days. Good for personal training clients.

### 7. HIPAA ✗ Not applicable

### 8. Pipeline Alias ✗ Missing
"fitness" business type not in _TYPE_ALIASES.

## Gaps Found
1. **No fitness pipeline preset** — Need: Trial → Consulted → Member → At Risk → Churned
2. **No liability waiver form preset** — Critical for gyms, legally required
3. **No class schedule integration** — Members can book PT but no group class booking
4. **No member retention tracking** — No way to detect "at risk" members (haven't visited in 30 days)
5. **No trial-to-member conversion tracking** — Key metric for fitness businesses

## Strengths
- Service types work for PT sessions (30min, 60min)
- Rebook at 30 days catches lapsed PT clients
- Aftercare email is motivational ("Stay hydrated, stretch")
- Booking flow works for PT and consultations

## Verdict
A boutique fitness studio would pay for basic PT booking + follow-up automation. The waiver form is a legal gap that needs fixing. Pipeline preset would help track trial→member conversion. Score: 7/10 (needs waiver form + pipeline to be 8/10).
