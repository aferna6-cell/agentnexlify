# Customer Simulation: Salon / Spa
Date: 2026-03-21

## Persona
Maria Lopez, salon owner. 10 years experience, 4 stylists, 2 nail techs. Uses Instagram heavily for before/after photos. Pain points: no-shows (costs $50-100/slot), clients booking the wrong service duration, new clients not knowing the price list.

## Journey
1. **Signup** — "salon" or "beauty" type selected. 6 FAQs auto-generated (services, booking, walk-ins, cancellation, pricing, bridal). Good coverage.
2. **Widget chat** — AI answers service questions well. Can direct to booking. Works for general inquiries.
3. **Booking** — Service types feature (Cycle 125) allows defining services with durations. Clients can pick "Haircut (30min)" vs "Full Color (120min)". Service type public endpoint exists for widget.
4. **Reminders** — 24h reminder includes "Arrive 5-10 minutes early" + "Photos of desired style". Rebook automation sends suggestion after 42 days. Both working.
5. **Pipeline** — Auto-seeds salon stages: New Client → Consulted → Booked → Regular Client → Inactive. Good match.
6. **Follow-up** — Welcome email sequence works. No salon-specific sequences (e.g., "birthday discount").
7. **Reviews** — One-click review request works. Good for building Google presence.
8. **Content** — Can generate social posts for before/after photos, promotions.

## Gaps Found
1. **No before/after photo gallery** — Stylists want to show their work. Content Studio exists but no portfolio-style gallery. Minor gap.
2. **No product recommendations** — After a color treatment, recommend products. Could be an automation.
3. **No birthday/anniversary automation** — "Happy birthday! Enjoy 10% off your next visit." Common salon feature.
4. **No waitlist for fully booked days** — If all slots are taken, no way to join a waitlist.
5. **No tipping integration** — Salons rely on tips. No way to add gratuity to invoices.

## Strengths
- Service types with custom durations (perfect for salon)
- Rebook automation at 42 days (matches salon revisit cycle)
- Pipeline preset matches salon workflow
- Reminder with "bring photos of desired style" is a nice touch
- Widget booking with service selection works well
- Review requests help build Google presence

## Verdict
A salon owner would pay for this TODAY. The booking with service types, rebook automation, and reminders cover the core needs. The gaps (birthday automation, waitlist, tips) are nice-to-haves, not deal-breakers. This is one of the best-fit business types for the platform.
