# Customer Simulation: Contractors / Home Services
Date: 2026-04-20

## Buyer Positioning
Turn estimate requests, callbacks, and repairs into booked jobs.

What the pack promises:
- Capture the right job details up front
- Respond quickly to urgent service requests
- Keep every estimate moving until it is scheduled
- Follow up after the job so reviews and reactivation do not get missed

## Default Widget Greeting
Hi! Thanks for reaching out to {{business_name}}. Are you looking for a repair, an estimate, or help with an existing job?

## 90-Second Onboarding Fields
Use these fields to get a contractor live fast without a long setup session:

1. Business name
2. Primary trade or service type
3. Service area or ZIP codes
4. Main phone number
5. Website URL
6. Top 3 services
7. Emergency service yes/no
8. Business hours
9. Preferred widget greeting

## Lead Fields
These are the lead details that matter most for home-service jobs:

1. Name
2. Phone
3. Email
4. Service needed
5. Property address
6. Job type
7. Timeline / urgency
8. Budget range
9. Photos or video link

## Automation Presets
The pack should feel useful immediately after onboarding:

1. Welcome series for new leads
2. Appointment reminder before site visits
3. Post-job review request
4. Invoice follow-up for overdue bills
5. 12-month reactivation for past customers

## FAQ Seed Set
Seeded FAQs should answer the questions contractors hear most:

- Do you offer free estimates?
- Are you licensed and insured?
- Do you offer a warranty on your work?
- What's your service area?
- Do you offer emergency service?
- What should I send before the estimate?
- Do you require a deposit?

## Demo Conversation Transcript
This transcript is meant to mirror the pack's default tone and lead capture behavior.

Visitor: Hi, I need an estimate for a bathroom remodel.

AI: Absolutely. Is this for a repair, replacement, or full remodel?

Visitor: Full remodel. It's a 1970s house and we want new tile, vanity, and lighting.

AI: Got it. What's the property address and when would you like someone to come take a look?

Visitor: 123 Maple St. We're flexible, but this month would be great.

AI: Perfect. I can book a site visit and note that you'd like a remodel estimate this month. Do you want phone, text, or email updates?

Visitor: Text is best.

AI: Done. You're on the calendar and our team will follow up with the estimate details.

## Notes
- This pack is the contractor-facing version of the home-services preset.
- It is a good fit for plumbing, roofing, electrical, HVAC, remodeling, landscaping, and similar trades.
- The practical next step for a partner is to seed the pack, then swap in their own services, hours, and estimate workflow details.
