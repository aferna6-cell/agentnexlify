## How it works (3 steps)

1. **Generate a key.** In Settings → Integrations → Zapier, create an API key.
   It is shown once; you copy it into Zapier.
2. **Pick your trigger and action.** Choose the AgentNexLiFy "New Lead" trigger,
   then the "Create Client / Lead / Customer" action for your CRM.
3. **Turn it on.** Every new lead flows into your CRM automatically, checked
   once a minute, never entered twice.

Speed to lead wins jobs. A lead that sits in a second system while you are on a
job is a lead your competitor answers first. AgentNexLiFy captures the lead the
moment a visitor asks, and Zapier puts it in the tools you already run — so the
follow-up happens on your schedule, from your CRM, without you touching a

## FAQ

**Do I need to be technical?** No. If you can copy a key and pick two dropdowns
in Zapier, you can set this up in a few minutes.

**Which CRMs are supported?** Anything Zapier connects to — 6,000+ apps —
featuring Jobber, ServiceTitan, and Housecall Pro for home services.

**Will a lead ever get entered twice?** No. Zapier de-duplicates on the lead's
unique id, so each lead is created once.

**What if I lose my key?** Revoke it and generate a new one. Keys are stored
encrypted and are never shown again after creation, so rotation is the fix.
