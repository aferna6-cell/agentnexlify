## Why it worked

The AI answers from MTOptions' own knowledge base, so responses cover their
actual alerts, membership tiers, and policies instead of generic replies.
Every conversation that shows buying intent gets asked for contact details,
and 15 of the month's 159 messages arrived outside business hours when nobody
was at a desk.
