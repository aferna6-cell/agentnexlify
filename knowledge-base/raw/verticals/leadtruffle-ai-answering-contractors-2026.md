# 6 Best AI Answering Services for Contractors in 2026

**Author:** Bryan Shankman
**Date:** January 22, 2026

## Overview
AI answering services qualify leads automatically while contractors are on the job. Traditional services charge $1-2/min and lack trade expertise; AI offers predictable monthly pricing + industry-aware qualification.

Core challenge: **"Miss that call, and there's an 85% chance that customer is calling your competitor next."**

## Essential Features
- Trade-specific qualification (water heater sizes vs generic)
- Service area filtering
- Emergency detection
- Field service integration (Jobber, ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro)
- After-hours handling (78% of customers buy from first responder)

**Optional:** missed-call text-back, appointment booking, photo collection via text, multilingual, voicemail transcription.

## ROI Math: Why AI Answering Makes Financial Sense

**Without AI Answering:**
- 100 calls received → 40 missed
- 85% of missed don't leave voicemail
- 75% never call back
- Avg job: $500
- Lost: 34 × $500 = **$17,000/mo**

**With AI Answering:**
- 100% of calls/messages handled
- 95% capture rate
- 25% conversion to appointments
- **$12,500 captured revenue**

**ROI on $229/mo: 5,400%**. Even one recovered job/month justifies investment.

## Selection Framework
- **Multi-source lead capture** → LeadTruffle (web, phone, Thumbtack/Angi/Yelp/Google LSA)
- **Budget testing** → Goodcall, Dialzara
- **Human backup** → Smith.ai, AnswerForce
- **Beginners** → My AI Front Desk, Goodcall

**Volume:**
- <50 calls/mo: flat-rate
- 50-200/mo: tiered (LeadTruffle)
- 200+/mo: custom/unlimited rates

**Integration priority:** ServiceTitan, Jobber, Housecall Pro = manual data entry elimination.
