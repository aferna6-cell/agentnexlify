## Frequently Asked Questions

### Are AI chatbots HIPAA-compliant?

Compliance depends on the platform and configuration. Many platforms offer HIPAA-compliant options with proper BAA (Business Associate Agreement), encryption, and access controls. Always verify compliance certifications before deployment.

### Will AI chatbots give medical advice?

Quality healthcare AI chatbots are configured with strict guardrails to avoid medical advice. They handle administrative tasks (scheduling, directions, insurance questions) and are programmed to escalate clinical questions to qualified staff.

### How do patients respond to AI communication?

Studies show high patient satisfaction with AI chatbots when they provide quick, accurate responses to routine questions. The key is transparency—let patients know they're interacting with AI and provide easy access to human support for complex needs.

### Can AI chatbots integrate with our EHR?

Most AI chatbot platforms offer API integration capabilities. The depth of integration varies—from basic appointment request capture to real-time EHR data access. Discuss your specific integration needs with vendors.

### What about elderly patients who aren't tech-savvy?

AI chatbots don't replace phone or in-person communication—they supplement it. Elderly patients can still call or visit, while tech-comfortable patients get self-service options. This actually improves phone availability for those who need it.
