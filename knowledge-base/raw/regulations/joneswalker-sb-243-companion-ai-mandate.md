# California's SB 243: AI Companion Chatbot Safety Mandate

**Publication:** October 15, 2025
**Author:** Jason M. Loring, Jones Walker LLP

## Overview
Governor Gavin Newsom signed Senate Bill 243 into law October 13, 2025 — California is "the first state to mandate specific safety safeguards for AI companion chatbots used by minors." Effective January 1, 2026, following public concerns about teen self-harm and suicide linked to conversational AI.

## Core Requirements

### Disclosure Standards
"Clear and conspicuous notification" that users interact with AI rather than humans. For minors:
- Break reminders at least every 3 hours
- Disclose AI nature
- Warn that companion chatbots "may not be suitable for some minors"

### Safety Protocols
Maintain protocols preventing suicidal ideation, suicide, or self-harm content generation. When users express such concerns, refer to crisis services. Implement safeguards against sexually explicit material directed toward minors. Use "evidence-based methods for measuring suicidal ideation."

### Reporting Obligations
From July 1, 2027, annual reports to California's Department of Public Health detailing crisis referral notifications and protocols for detecting/responding to suicidal ideation.

## Enforcement Provisions
Private right of action. Injured parties may seek:
- Injunctive relief
- Damages of either actual losses or **$1,000 per violation**, whichever is greater
- Attorney fees

## Legislative Support
Overwhelming bipartisan backing:
- Senate: **33-3**
- Assembly: **59-1**

Signals broad consensus on youth digital safety priorities.
