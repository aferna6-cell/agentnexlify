## 6-Step GEO Implementation Process

1. **Audit AI visibility & foundations (30-45 min)** — HubSpot free AEO Grader; search business name + service in ChatGPT/Perplexity/Google AI Overviews; complete Google Business Profile; Google Rich Results Test; PageSpeed Insights.
2. **Research LLM citation patterns (1-2 hrs)** — search target customer questions in each AI tool; note which competitors get cited; analyze their content structure, credentials, Q&A formats, schema. Tools: AnswerThePublic, "People Also Ask", sales/CS feedback.
3. **Optimize existing content for LLM readability** — direct answer in first 200-300 words; business name + service + location in first paragraph; descriptive H2/H3s; comparison tables; FAQ sections with schema; credibility signals (author credentials, dates, review counts, certifications, response times, service areas, pricing ranges).
4. **Plan question-led content with entity coverage** — list 10-15 core customer questions; fill gaps; prioritize by volume + business impact. Entity coverage = being specific about who/what/where ("Ramona's Elite Events offers party planning in downtown Boston" vs "we offer services").
5. **Multi-platform publishing** — Google Business Profile (primary), YouTube, LinkedIn, industry directories (NAP consistency), local news/blogs, review sites.
6. **Monthly measurement** — number of AI citations, AI referral traffic (GA/HubSpot), citation positioning vs competitors; HubSpot AEO tracks across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini.

## 5 Core GEO Best Practices

1. **Prioritize Google Business Profile** — WhiteSpark: most critical ranking factor for small/local businesses. Complete all sections; post weekly; photos = 35% more engagement; respond to every review within 24-48 hours.
2. **Essential schema markup** — LocalBusiness, FAQ, and Service schema. Free tools: Google Structured Data Markup Helper, Schema Markup Generator, CMS plugins.
3. **Question-focused FAQ pages** — 100-200 word answers, explicit FAQ schema, specific details (prices, timelines, service areas, credentials), links to service pages.
4. **Leverage customer reviews systematically** — prompt for detailed reviews mentioning specific services and outcomes; richer reviews carry more AI weight; embed with Review Schema; respond within 24-48 hours. FCC note: late-2024 rules permit asking for experience sharing but prohibit requesting specific star ratings or offering review incentives.
5. **NAP consistency** — character-for-character identical name/address/phone across all platforms; inconsistency can exclude a business from citations entirely. 2-3 hours audit, 30 min quarterly.

## Local GEO Sub-Tactics

- Location-specific content pages (neighborhood names, landmarks, ZIPs, local testimonials, photos)
- Local backlinks: 2-3 quality links/month (local news expert pitches, event sponsorship, Chamber of Commerce, local roundups)
- "Near me" voice search optimization: conversational structure, long-tail keywords, hours prominent, fast mobile
- Locally-focused blog content addressing local pain points (e.g. "Noise Ordinance Requirements in Boston")
- Local online communities: Nextdoor, local Facebook groups, local subreddits, GBP Q&A — genuine expertise, not promo spam

## Tooling & Budget

**Free:** Google Search Console; HubSpot Marketing Hub free tier (+free AEO Grader); Screaming Frog free tier (500 URLs); AnswerThePublic (3 searches/day).
**Paid:** HubSpot Starter $20/mo; Professional $890/mo; HubSpot AEO $50/mo standalone (tracks brand visibility across ChatGPT/Perplexity/Gemini, competitor share of voice); Screaming Frog paid $259/yr; AnswerThePublic Pro $99/mo.
**Maintenance:** 3-5 hours/month in-house; $0-50/month for first 6-12 months. Agency route: $1,500-5,000/month; consultant middle option: $500-1,500 initial strategy + $300-800/month check-ins.

## GEO vs AEO

HubSpot prefers "AEO" (answer engine optimization); both = getting cited in AI-generated answers; tactics overlap heavily with SEO — question-focused content + schema strengthens both.
