# GEO Guide 2026: Generative Engine Optimization Explained

*Digital Applied. Jan 2, 2026.*

## Framing

- 47% of brands have no GEO strategy.
- Top-performing GEO methods lift AI-search visibility **+30–40%**.

| | SEO | AEO | GEO |
|---|---|---|---|
| Target | Ranked links | Featured/direct answers | Citations inside generated answers |
| Engine | Google SERP | Snippets, voice | ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini, AI Overviews |
| Win condition | Position 1–3 | Answer box | Being cited / named in the response |

## Princeton GEO study — content tactics and measured lift

| Tactic | Visibility lift |
|---|---|
| Cite sources | +40% |
| Add statistics | +37% |
| Include quotations | +30% |
| Use technical terms | +28% |

## Ten strategies

1. Cite authoritative sources inline
2. Lead with statistics
3. Include expert quotes
4. Use precise domain terminology
5. Give clear definitions
6. Descriptive, question-shaped headings
7. FAQ blocks
8. Tables for comparisons
9. Visible freshness dates (updated YYYY-MM-DD)
10. Topical depth — cover the whole cluster, not one page

## Measurement: Share of Model (SoM)

```
SoM = (your brand citations / total citations) × 100
```

Run 20–50 target queries monthly across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini; record who gets cited.

## Timeline expectations

3–6 months. Target **10–20% SoM by month 3**, **30–40% by month 6** in your niche queries.

## Mistakes

Ignoring AI search entirely; unsourced claims; burying the answer below the fold; optimizing for one platform; quitting before month 3.
