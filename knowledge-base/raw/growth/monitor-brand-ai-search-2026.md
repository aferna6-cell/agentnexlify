# How to Monitor Your Brand Across ChatGPT, Perplexity and AI Search

**Published:** August 24, 2026

## The Measurement Gap

Rank tracking answers "where do I appear for this keyword." AI search does not have keywords or ranks — it has prompts and synthesized answers. A brand can be losing enormous ground in AI-assisted buying decisions while its keyword rankings look untouched.

The gap is worst for consideration-stage queries, where users increasingly ask an assistant "who's the best X for Y" instead of running a search and clicking through comparisons.

## What to Actually Measure

Five metrics, in order of usefulness:

### 1. Mention Rate
Percentage of tracked prompts where your brand is mentioned at all. The most basic signal — are you in the conversation.

### 2. Citation Rate
Percentage of prompts where your brand is mentioned **with a link to your domain**. Mentions without citations mean the model knows you but is not sourcing you — a different problem with a different fix.

### 3. Share of Voice
Your mentions as a proportion of all brand mentions across the tracked prompt set. This is the competitive metric, and the one that moves in response to work.

### 4. Sentiment and Framing
How you are described. Being mentioned as "the budget option" or "harder to set up" is a mention you may not want. Track the adjectives.

### 5. Cited Page Distribution
Which of your pages get cited. Reveals what content type the engines find useful and where to invest.

## Building the Prompt Set

The prompt set is the entire measurement instrument — a bad one produces meaningless numbers.

Construction method:

1. **Start from real customer language**, not keyword tools. Sales call transcripts, support tickets, and the questions prospects actually ask.
2. **Cover the funnel:**
   - Problem-aware: "how do I stop missing calls at my plumbing business"
   - Solution-aware: "what is an AI receptionist"
   - Vendor-aware: "best AI receptionist software for contractors"
   - Comparison: "X vs Y"
   - Objection: "is an AI receptionist HIPAA compliant"
3. **Include geography** where relevant: "AI answering service for dentists in Boston"
4. **Include your brand name explicitly** in a few prompts to test what the engines say about you directly
5. **Include competitor names** to see when you surface in their consideration set
6. **Target 50–150 prompts.** Fewer produces noisy percentages; more gets expensive to run at cadence.

Freeze the set for a measurement period. Changing prompts between runs destroys comparability.

## Running the Measurement

**Manual approach** — viable at small scale. Run each prompt in each engine in a fresh, logged-out session, record the results in a spreadsheet. Roughly 2–4 hours per 100 prompts per engine.

Critical hygiene:
- **Logged out and personalization off.** Your own history biases results toward your brand.
- **Consistent geography.** Location changes local answers dramatically.
- **Same time window.** Answers shift; comparing a Monday run to a Friday run introduces noise.
- **Fresh session per prompt.** Conversational context carries over and contaminates the next result.

**Automated approach** — a growing tool category runs prompt sets across engines on a schedule and reports the metrics above. Evaluate on: which engines are covered, whether geographic variation is supported, whether raw answer text is retained (not just a score), and export access to the underlying data.

**DIY approach** — API access to each engine plus a scheduler. Cheapest at scale, but note API-served answers can differ from the consumer interface, so validate the delta before trusting it.

## Cadence

- **Monthly** is the right default for most businesses — enough signal, manageable cost
- **Weekly** during an active optimization push or after a major content release
- **Ad hoc** after any significant event: launch, rebrand, negative press, competitor launch

Establish a baseline before making changes. Without it, no subsequent number means anything.

## Interpreting Results

Common patterns and what they indicate:

| Pattern | Likely cause | Fix |
|---------|--------------|-----|
| Mentioned but never cited | Model knows the brand from training data; site content is not retrievable or extractable | Structure content for extraction; ensure crawlability |
| Cited only on brand-name prompts | No topical authority on category questions | Publish specific answers to category questions |
| Competitors dominate comparison prompts | They have comparison content; you do not | Publish honest comparison pages |
| Wrong facts stated about you | Stale or inconsistent information across the web | Correct source pages, directories, and profiles |
| Strong on one engine, absent on another | Engine-specific weighting — freshness vs authority vs entity data | Diagnose per engine and optimize separately |
| Local prompts miss you entirely | Business profile and directory data incomplete | Fix entity data before touching content |

## Acting on the Data

The measurement is only useful if it drives a queue of work:

1. Rank prompts by commercial value, not by how badly you do on them
2. For each high-value prompt where you are absent, identify what the cited sources have that you do not
3. Produce the missing content in the format the engines are actually citing
4. Fix factual errors at the source — engines propagate what they find
5. Re-measure in the next cycle and attribute movement

## What Not to Do

- Do not chase every prompt — most have no commercial value
- Do not judge from a single run; answers vary between runs for the same prompt
- Do not measure logged in as yourself
- Do not treat AI referral traffic as the whole picture — most AI-influenced decisions produce no measurable click
- Do not change the prompt set mid-measurement-period

## Bottom Line

AI search visibility is measurable, but only with a deliberate instrument: a fixed, funnel-covering prompt set, run under controlled conditions, on a fixed cadence, tracking mention rate, citation rate, share of voice, sentiment, and cited-page distribution. Everything downstream — content strategy, entity cleanup, comparison pages — is prioritized by that data.
