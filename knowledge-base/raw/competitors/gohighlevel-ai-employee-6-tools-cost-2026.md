# GoHighLevel AI Employee 2026: All 6 Tools + Real Cost

**Published:** 23 Mar 2026

## Overview

GoHighLevel AI Employee comprises five AI-powered tools automating business communications and marketing tasks: Voice AI, Conversation AI, Reviews AI, Content AI, and Funnel AI.

## The Five Components

### 1. Voice AI (AI Voice Agents)
Handles inbound calls with natural conversation, answers FAQs, books appointments, routes complex calls to humans. Supports 10 languages for transcription as of March 2026.

- Natural conversational voice responses
- Direct calendar booking
- After-hours call handling
- Automatic call transcription

Use cases: medical offices, HVAC services, real estate, restaurants.

### 2. Conversation AI (Chat and SMS Bot)
Powers website chat widgets and SMS automation, qualifying leads and booking appointments. Two modes: suggestive (human-reviewed) or auto-pilot (fully autonomous).

### 3. Reviews AI
Automates review request sequences, monitors new reviews across platforms, generates response drafts for Google reviews.

### 4. Content AI
Writing assistant integrated throughout the platform for emails, SMS, blog posts, social captions, funnel copy.

### 5. Funnel AI and Websites AI
Generates complete page structures from text prompts, reducing funnel setup from 3-4 hours to 30-60 minutes of refinement.

## 2026 Pricing

**AI Employee Unlimited Plan: $97/month**

Includes unlimited use of Conversation AI, Reviews AI, Content AI, Funnel AI (fair use applies). Voice AI with native GHL model included; external voice models billed separately at $0.05–$0.15/minute.

This is **separate** from base platform subscription ($97–$497/month).

## How Agencies Package It

- AI Receptionist Package: $200–$400/month (Voice AI only)
- Full AI Employee Package: $300–$600/month (Voice AI + Conversation AI + Reviews AI)
- Reputation Management Add-on: $100–$200/month (Reviews AI standalone)

## Realistic Expectations

Works well: after-hours call capture, FAQ handling for predictable questions, appointment booking, call routing.

Still requires humans: complex customer service, highly variable pricing scenarios, medical or sensitive conversations.

Most deployments handle 70–80% of contacts autonomously, routing the remainder to humans.
