## SMS and Carrier Details

- One segment = 160 plain-text characters. Emojis or non-Latin characters drop the limit to 70 chars/segment — "one emoji turns a 160-character message from one segment into three."
- Inbound SMS billed at the same rate as outbound. "A reply from a Verizon number costs 21% more than the message that prompted it. On US Cellular the gap is 40%."
- MMS: $0.0220 + carrier fees ($0.0070–$0.0100) ≈ $0.031/message, ~3x plain text.
- International: Australia ≈ $0.0515 per segment, six times US rates.

## AI Employee Pricing Structure

Both Growth ($50) and Unlimited ($97) tiers charge **per enabled sub-account**, not per agency. Ten sub-accounts on Unlimited = $970/month — more than the $497 Agency Pro plan. Phone charges apply independently to all AI calls regardless of tier.

## Rebilling and Margins

- Unlimited: rebill phone and email usage at cost.
- Agency Pro: custom markup multipliers (1.5x and 2x documented). At 2x, the 15-sub-account agency turns $627.10 of monthly usage into $627.10 of monthly margin.

## Excluded Costs

WhatsApp ($10/sub-account/month + per-message), workflow premium executions ($0.01 each), call recording/transcription ($0.0025–$0.024/min), add-ons: SEO ($79), HIPAA ($297), branded portal ($49), premium support ($500), white-label app ($497).

Wallet auto-recharge cannot be disabled. Exceeding three recharges in seven days auto-moves you to the next tier.

## Recommendation Framework

- Starter: three or fewer businesses
- Unlimited: four+ sub-accounts or cost-pass-through
- Agency Pro: only if reselling with markup

All plans include a 14-day free trial; telecom usage fees still apply during trial.
