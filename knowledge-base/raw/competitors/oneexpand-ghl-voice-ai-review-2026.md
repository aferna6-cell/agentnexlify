What Is GoHighLevel Voice AI?
GoHighLevel Voice AI is an AI-powered virtual receptionist built natively inside the GoHighLevel platform. Unlike traditional IVR systems that force callers to press buttons, Voice AI conducts real, natural-sounding conversations with your callers.
It is part of the broader
AI Employee suite
— GoHighLevel’s flagship 2025–2026 feature update — which also includes Conversation AI (SMS and chat), Reviews AI, Content AI, and Workflow AI. Voice AI specifically handles inbound phone calls.
What does it actually do on a call?
When a call comes in to your assigned number, the Voice AI agent answers immediately — usually within two rings. It then:
Greets the caller with a custom script you write
Asks qualifying questions to understand what the caller needs
Collects caller information (name, email, address) and updates your CRM automatically
Books appointments directly into your connected calendar via workflow triggers
Transfers the call to a human agent if a pre-set condition is met
Sends follow-up SMS messages after the call ends
Logs a full call transcript to the contact record in GHL

How GoHighLevel Voice AI Works: Setup Walkthrough
Voice AI operates through GHL’s
Agent Studio
. Here is how to set it up from scratch:
1
Enable AI Employee in Agency Settings
Navigate to Agency Settings → Company tab → scroll down to AI Employee and toggle it on. Enable it for the specific sub-accounts you want to use it in.
2
Open Agent Studio
Inside your sub-account, go to AI Employee → Agent Studio. Click “Create New Agent” and select Voice Agent as the type.
3
Choose a Voice
Select from male or female voices in professional or casual tones. You can preview each voice before selecting. The 2026 voices are noticeably more natural than earlier versions.
4
Write the Agent’s Goal and Script
Example:
“Your name is Sarah. You are a friendly receptionist for [Business Name]. Your goal is to greet callers warmly, find out what service they need, and book them into the calendar.”
5
Upload Your Knowledge Base
Instead of scripting every possible Q&A, upload your website URL, pricing PDF, or FAQ document. The AI reads it and answers caller questions about your business automatically.
6
Set Transfer Rules
Define when the AI should hand off to a human. If the caller says “emergency,” “complaint,” or “speak to a person,” the AI immediately transfers to your personal number.
7
Assign a Phone Number and Go Live
Assign any LC Phone or Twilio number to the agent. Set active business hours. Outside those hours, calls follow your fallback rules — voicemail, another number, or the AI itself.

Frequently Asked Questions
Does GoHighLevel Voice AI work with any phone number?
No. Voice AI only works with LC Phone numbers (GoHighLevel’s native phone system, powered by Twilio) or directly connected Twilio numbers. Standard landlines, Google Voice, and other VoIP providers are not supported.
How much does GoHighLevel Voice AI cost per month?
Token-based pricing costs approximately $0.06 per minute for voice processing plus LLM token usage, on top of regular per-minute phone charges (~$0.013–$0.021/min). The Unlimited AI Employees plan ($97/month per sub-account) removes per-usage limits for all AI Employee tools including Voice AI.
Can GoHighLevel Voice AI book appointments?
Yes, but through a specific process. The Voice AI agent collects appointment details during the call and then triggers a GHL workflow, which handles the actual calendar booking. You need to configure this workflow connection for appointment booking to work automatically.
Is GoHighLevel Voice AI better than Synthflow?
For existing GHL users, yes — because it is natively integrated with no extra tools or subscriptions required. For non-GHL users, Synthflow offers strong no-code voice automation with its own workflow builder.
Can I use multiple Voice AI agents in one GoHighLevel account?
Yes. You can create multiple AI Voice Agents in Agent Studio, each with its own phone number, unique voice, custom script, and separate knowledge base. Ideal for agencies managing multiple clients.
Does GoHighLevel Voice AI work in languages other than English?
As of early 2026, GoHighLevel Voice AI is primarily optimized for English. Non-English conversation quality is significantly lower. Synthflow (90+ languages) may be more suitable for multilingual needs.
