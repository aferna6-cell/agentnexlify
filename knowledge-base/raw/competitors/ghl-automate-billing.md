How to create predictable revenue and automatic subscriptions with HighLevel
Tired of chasing invoices every month? HighLevel makes it easy to automate billing by turning your services into recurring subscription plans. Instead of manually sending payment requests, you can set up automatic charges on a schedule that works for your business.
This means you spend less time on admin tasks and more time focusing on growth.
HighLevel recurring payments improve cash flow
When you use subscriptions, your revenue becomes predictable. You know what is coming in each month, which makes planning and scaling your business much easier.
Here’s why subscriptions are powerful:
✔ Consistent monthly income
✔ Reduced manual invoicing
✔ Better client retention through ongoing services
✔ Improved financial forecasting
HighLevel subscription setup step-by-step
Getting started with subscriptions inside HighLevel is straightforward:
1️⃣ Go to Payments in your sub-account
2️⃣ Click Products and create a new product
3️⃣ Choose recurring payment and set your pricing
4️⃣ Select your billing schedule such as monthly or yearly
5️⃣ Add an optional free trial if needed
6️⃣ Save your product
HighLevel subscription creation for customers
Once your product is ready, you can assign it to a customer:
1️⃣ Navigate to Subscriptions
2️⃣ Click Add Subscription
3️⃣ Select the customer
4️⃣ Choose your product
5️⃣ Add or save a payment method
6️⃣ Schedule the subscription
That’s it. Your billing is now fully automated.
HighLevel subscriptions support business growth
By switching to subscriptions, you create a more stable and scalable business model. No more chasing payments or worrying about inconsistent income. Instead, you build reliable revenue that grows over time.
