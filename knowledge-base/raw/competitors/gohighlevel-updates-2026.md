## April 2026 Updates

### Global Tax Compliance - Stripe Tax Integration (April 24, 2026)
GoHighLevel launched Global Tax Compliance powered by Stripe Tax: automatic tax collection, tax invoices, tax reports for sub-accounts across 100+ countries.
- US state-level sales tax based on Stripe Tax registrations
- EU VAT (OSS Union, OSS Non-Union, IOSS for digital services)
- Canada GST/HST/PST, APAC, LATAM, MEA
- Automatic jurisdiction sync, compliant invoices per sub-account
- Calculations only — agencies remain responsible for nexus, registration, remittance
- Setup: Agency Settings > SaaS Mode > Tax Configuration > Connect Stripe Tax

### Smart Lists HighRise UI Redesign (April 20, 2026)
Rebuilt with HighRise design system. Improved layout, performance, usability. Currently in Labs. Benefits accounts managing 50+ Smart Lists across multiple sub-accounts.

### LC Phone SMS Rate Update (April 16, 2026)
Rate increases (citing carrier cost increases):
- Bangladesh: +54% ($0.3869 → $0.5962)
- Angola: +18% ($0.2588 → $0.3062)
- Armenia: +18% ($0.2528 → $0.2996)
- Belgium: +6%, Chile: +7%
- US domestic: unchanged

### Revenue Forecasting in Opportunities (April 14, 2026)
Probability-weighted revenue projections with timeline views. Enable via Subaccount > Labs. Native pipeline forecasting without spreadsheet exports.

### Voice AI Without Number Purchase (April 9, 2026)
Voice AI now works with existing business phone numbers — no LC Phone purchase required. Lowers first-activation barrier; full test during 30-day trial.
