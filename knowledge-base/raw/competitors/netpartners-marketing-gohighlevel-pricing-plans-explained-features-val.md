## Starter Plan - $97/Month

The Starter plan is for a single business owner or a freelancer managing up to 3 accounts.

What you get:

3 sub-accounts

Full GoHighLevel feature set (not a limited version - same tools as higher tiers)

CRM with unlimited contacts and pipelines

Funnel and website builder

Email marketing and automation workflows

SMS and phone calling (usage billed separately - see LC Phone rates below)

Reputation management and review automation

Social media planner

Courses, communities, and membership hosting

Calendar and appointment booking

Who should pick Starter: A solo operator managing their own business, or a freelancer with 1-3 client accounts. You get the complete platform.

Who should not pick Starter: Anyone managing a 4th client account needs Unlimited. The sub-account cap is the only real restriction.

## Unlimited Plan - $297/Month

The agency standard. Removes the sub-account ceiling entirely.

What you get beyond Starter:

Unlimited sub-accounts

Branded desktop app (agency-level white-labeling of the interface)

API access for custom integrations

User-level reporting across accounts

Who should pick Unlimited: Any agency with 4 or more active client accounts. At $297/mo for unlimited sub-accounts, the per-client platform cost approaches zero as you scale.

The math: 20 clients on Unlimited = $14.85/client/month for platform access.

Who should not pick Unlimited: If you want to resell GoHighLevel to clients as a subscription product under your brand, you need Agency Pro. Unlimited does not include SaaS Mode or automated client billing.

## Is GoHighLevel Worth the Price?

At $97/mo Starter: yes, for any business replacing two or more tools.

At $297/mo Unlimited: yes, for any agency managing 4+ clients. The per-client cost is under $15/mo at 20 accounts.

At $497/mo Agency Pro: yes, but only if you have or can acquire 3+ paying SaaS clients. At 2 clients paying $297, you break even on the platform. Every client beyond that generates near-pure recurring margin.

At $497/mo + $970/mo AI Employee across 10 clients: yes, if you are packaging AI as a service. At $397/client × 10 clients you net $2,379/mo after all platform costs. The AI pays for itself if positioned correctly.

The honest case against GoHighLevel: if you only need email marketing and have under 500 contacts, ActiveCampaign at $49/mo is cheaper and simpler. GoHighLevel justifies its cost when you use 60%+ of the platform.

## GoHighLevel Pricing FAQ

How much does GoHighLevel cost per month? GoHighLevel costs $97/mo (Starter), $297/mo (Unlimited), or $497/mo (Agency Pro). These are base plan prices. Usage fees for SMS, calls, email, and AI tools are billed separately and typically add $20-$150/mo depending on volume.

What is GoHighLevel AI pricing in 2026? AI Employee is available pay-as-you-go or via an Unlimited plan at $97/mo per sub-account. Pay-as-you-go rates: Conversation AI at $0.02/message, Voice AI at approximately $0.163/min average (voice engine + LLM tokens combined), Reviews AI at $0.08/automated response. The $97/mo AI Employee Unlimited covers Conversation AI, Reviews AI, Content AI, and Funnel AI without per-usage caps. Voice AI with external models is billed additionally.

What is the difference between Unlimited and Agency Pro? Unlimited includes unlimited sub-accounts and API access but does not include SaaS Mode. Agency Pro adds SaaS Mode (automated client billing via Stripe), white-label mobile app, and reseller controls. If you plan to bill clients for GoHighLevel access under your own brand, Agency Pro is required.

Does GoHighLevel have hidden fees? Usage-based costs are real but not hidden - they are documented in GoHighLevel's support portal. LC Phone SMS and call rates, LC Email send costs, Workflow Pro Plan fees for premium actions, AI tool credits, and WordPress hosting all sit outside the base plan price. Light usage adds $20-$50/mo. Heavy AI usage at scale can add $500+/mo.

What is the annual discount on GoHighLevel? Annual billing reduces rates to approximately $81/mo (Starter), $248/mo (Unlimited), and $414/mo (Agency Pro). This saves roughly $192, $588, and $996 per year respectively compared to monthly billing.

What is the GoHighLevel free trial period? The standard GoHighLevel free trial is 14 days. The link in this post provides a 30-day free trial - twice the standard period - giving you time to build workflows, test AI tools, and validate ROI before committing.

What is A2P 10DLC and why does it affect costs? A2P 10DLC is the mandatory US carrier registration for business SMS. All GoHighLevel accounts sending texts to US numbers must register. Registration is $4-$19 one-time per brand. Without it, carriers block your messages. This is a regulatory requirement, not a GoHighLevel fee.

Can you upgrade or downgrade GoHighLevel plans? Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately. Downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle. Annual billing locks in the discounted rate for 12 months.

How do carrier fee increases in 2026 affect GoHighLevel pricing? AT&T increased carrier fees effective April 1, 2026. Verizon increases go into effect May 1, 2026. These changes apply to all US SMS traffic processed through GoHighLevel's LC Phone system. Per-message costs for affected carriers will be slightly higher than historical rates.

Is GoHighLevel worth $497/mo for Agency Pro? At $497/mo, breakeven requires 2 clients at $297/mo. Any agency with 3+ paying clients is net positive on platform cost alone. The value is the automated billing infrastructure and white-label controls - not just the feature set.
