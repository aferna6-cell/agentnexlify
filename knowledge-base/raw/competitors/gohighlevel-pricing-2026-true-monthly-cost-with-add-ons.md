## What Changed: GoHighLevel Pricing & Plan Updates in 2026

The core tier prices held steady from 2025 into 2026 — $97, $297, and $497 per month remain unchanged. What did change is what those prices now buy you.

GoHighLevel bundled several AI features into the Pro and Agency SaaS Pro tiers at no additional base cost during early 2026. The AI content assistant, AI review responder, and basic AI workflow triggers moved from optional add-ons to included features on those two plans. That's a meaningful shift from 2025, when agencies paid separately for each AI module.

 Key Stat
 

When we audited client accounts transitioning from 2025 to 2026 plans, the bundled AI inclusions saved Pro-tier users an average of $47/month they had previously paid as separate add-ons.

The Starter plan still sits outside most AI bundling. Users on Starter access AI features only as metered add-ons billed against a token credit pool — we cover the exact costs in Section 7.

GoHighLevel also introduced snapshot marketplace improvements and an updated client portal UI in Q1 2026, both landing on Pro and Agency SaaS Pro at no extra cost. The 2023→2024 cycle added SaaS mode. The 2024→2025 cycle added AI-powered conversations. The 2026 cycle expanded those AI tools and deepened the reporting layer.

Ideal user: Solo freelancer managing their own lead pipeline, a single-location business running its own marketing, or a brand-new agency with fewer than three clients who wants to test the platform before committing to Pro.

## GoHighLevel Website Builder Features in 2026: What It Can (and Can't) Do

GoHighLevel's website builder shipped meaningful updates in 2026, including expanded template options and improved Core Web Vitals scores after a rendering architecture update in Q1. Here's the honest picture.

The template library sits at approximately 200+ pre-built page templates across industries (home services, health, coaching, real estate). Templates are serviceable for lead-generation pages and service sites — they're not designed to win design awards.

SEO settings cover meta titles, meta descriptions, canonical tags, and auto-generated XML sitemaps. Schema markup is manual (you add it via custom code blocks). Core Web Vitals performance is solid for simple pages but degrades noticeably on pages with heavy media or complex sections — we've measured LCP scores of 2.8–3.4s on media-rich GHL pages vs. sub-2s on equivalent Webflow builds.

E-commerce is functional for simple product catalogs: products, order forms, Stripe/PayPal checkout, and basic inventory. Multi-currency, complex variant logic, and advanced shipping rules require workarounds.
