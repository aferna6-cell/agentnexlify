# Manus AI Comes to GoHighLevel: Full Breakdown of the 2026 GHL Integrations

## Manus AI Comes to GoHighLevel: Full Breakdown of the 2026 GHL Integrations

GoHighLevel continues to evolve as one of the most powerful all in one marketing platforms for agencies, SaaS resellers, and automation builders. The February 2026 update introduced several major integrations that significantly expand what you can automate inside HighLevel.

The most talked about feature is the Manus AI integration. Alongside that, we also got native [Monday.com](http://Monday.com) integration, Google Forms automation support, improved calendar settings, and a prospecting pipeline upgrade inside sub accounts.

In this guide, we will break down:

• What Manus AI inside GoHighLevel actually does
• How to use it for lead research and CRM enrichment
• How [Monday.com](http://Monday.com) now connects directly to workflows
• How Google Forms can trigger automations
• What changed in calendar settings
• How prospecting now pushes directly into pipelines

If you are running an agency, managing leads, or reselling HighLevel, these updates matter.

## 1. Manus AI Integration Inside GoHighLevel

This is the headline feature.

Manus AI can now connect directly to GoHighLevel workflows using API integration. That means you can trigger AI tasks inside your automation flows and then pull the results back into your CRM.

If you run Meta Ads or handle inbound leads, this is where things get interesting.

### What Manus AI Can Do Inside GHL

Manus AI acts like a junior analyst that can:

• Research inbound leads
• Scrape website data
• Enrich Google Contacts
• Generate proposals
• Create internal notes based on analysis

One powerful use case is inbound lead enrichment.

Imagine someone submits a form on your website. Instead of your sales team going in blind, HighLevel can:
- 

Trigger a Manus AI task

- 

Send lead data to Manus

- 

Wait for processing

- 

Pull the output back into the CRM

- 

Create an internal note

- 

Notify your sales rep

By the time your rep reaches out, they already know who the lead is, what their company does, and relevant context.

That changes the quality of your outreach.

### How to Connect Manus AI to GoHighLevel

There are two main ways:

• Through Settings → Integrations
• Through the Workflow Builder

You will need your Manus API key. Once connected, you can add Manus triggers or actions directly inside workflows.

Important detail: Manus actions count as premium workflow actions. If you are reselling GHL, factor this into your pricing.

Also important: AI credits are not included through HighLevel. AI usage is billed directly through Manus.

This is not free automation. It is leverage.

## 2. [Monday.com](http://Monday.com) Integration with GoHighLevel

If you manage clients or internal teams, this is a serious upgrade.

Previously, you needed Zapier or Make to connect [Monday.com](http://Monday.com) to HighLevel. Now it is native.

You can:

• Create new items in Monday boards
• Update items
• Delete items
• Create boards and sub items
• Trigger workflows based on status changes

### Agency Use Case

Let’s say you close a new client in your HighLevel pipeline.

You can automatically:

• Create a new onboarding board item in Monday
• Assign it to your team
• Notify account managers
• Send welcome emails
• Update CRM status

Or the reverse.

If a project is marked complete in Monday, you can:

• Trigger an email to the client
• Send a Slack notification
• Update pipeline stage

This removes the need for third party automation tools and keeps everything inside GHL workflows.

Like Manus AI, [Monday.com](http://Monday.com) actions are premium workflow actions.

If you are serious about systemizing your agency, this reduces friction between operations and CRM.

## 3. Google Forms Native Integration

This one solves a real pain point.

Before this update, if you used Google Forms for questionnaires, onboarding, or feedback, you had two options:

• Manual CSV imports
• Third party tools

Now Google Forms connects directly to GoHighLevel workflows.

### What You Can Do

When a new Google Form response is submitted, you can:

• Create or update a contact in GHL
• Trigger email sequences
• Send SMS follow ups
• Notify sales reps
• Tag contacts
• Launch conditional automations

You can select:

• The specific form
• Start date for pulling responses
• Maximum response limit

This is ideal for:

• Client onboarding forms
• Lead qualification surveys
• NPS collection
• Feedback campaigns

Every submission instantly becomes a CRM contact with automated follow up.

No CSV files. No extra tools.

Again, this is a premium workflow action.

## 4. Improved Calendar Settings Experience

This is not flashy, but it improves usability.

The calendar settings page is now:

• Wider
• Cleaner
• Better structured
• Easier to navigate

You now have clearer sections for:

• Staff availability
• Booking rules
• Forms and confirmations
• Payments
• Notifications
• Policies
• Booking channels
• Widget customization

If you build funnels or appointment systems inside GoHighLevel, this makes configuration smoother and faster.

Better layout equals fewer mistakes.

## 5. Prospecting Tool Pipeline Integration

This is one of the most practical updates for agencies doing outbound.

You can now push prospects directly into a pipeline stage inside your sub account.

Here is how it works:
- 

Go into your sub account

- 

Navigate to Marketing → Prospecting

- 

Select a prospect

- 

Enable Add to Pipeline

- 

Choose pipeline and stage

Once added, you can:

• Trigger automations
• Send outreach emails
• Launch follow up sequences
• Track opportunity source

You can now track exactly how many deals come from:

• Facebook Ads
• Organic search
• Prospecting
• Referrals

This gives you better attribution data and smarter decision making.

More data equals better optimization.

## Why These 2026 Updates Matter

GoHighLevel is moving toward becoming a central automation engine.

With:

• AI enrichment
• Native project management sync
• Direct Google Forms connection
• Improved CRM prospecting flow

You reduce tool stack complexity.

If you are an agency owner, this means:

• Fewer third party tools
• Fewer integration failures
• More centralized automation
• Better client experience

But here is the reality.

These features only create leverage if you build structured workflows. If your CRM is messy and your pipelines are undefined, adding AI will not fix your system.

Automation multiplies structure. It does not replace it.

The [Monday.com](http://Monday.com) integration strengthens operational automation.

The Google Forms update removes friction in data collection.

The calendar improvements increase usability.

The prospecting pipeline integration strengthens outbound sales management.

If you are serious about scaling with GoHighLevel in 2026, these updates are worth implementing now before your competitors do.
