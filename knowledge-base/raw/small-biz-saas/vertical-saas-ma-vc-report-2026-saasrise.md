# Vertical SaaS M&A and VC Report 2026

*Author: Ryan Allis (SaaS Rise). May 28, 2026. Covers 1,275+ M&A transactions and 2,300+ VC rounds, Jan 2025 – May 2026.*

## Headline multiples

- Vertical SaaS trades at a **41% premium** to horizontal: **5.8× vs 4.1×** median M&A EV/ARR.
- 2026 YTD: vertical **5.5×** vs horizontal **3.4×**.
- By vertical (M&A median): healthcare IT 8.5×, construction 7.5×, legal 7.0×, field services 7.0×.
- VC round medians: 8.5× overall; healthcare 12.5×; legal 10×.

## Eight trends

1. **AI accelerates verticalization** — AI-native vertical companies grow 2–3× faster and command a 25–30% premium.
2. **System of record → system of execution** — buyers pay for software that does the work, not just stores it.
3. **Embedded fintech is the #1 revenue lever** — 87% of top vertical SaaS include payments; 2–5× revenue per customer.
4. **Multi-product is mandatory** — second and third products drive ~10× logo expansion.
5. **Consolidation** — PE accounts for 58% of deals.
6. **Hot verticals**: healthcare, legal, construction, insurance, field services.
7. **Usage / outcome pricing** — 61% of vertical SaaS now use some usage-based component.
8. **AI agents unbundle the UI** — value shifts from dashboards to agents that act.

## Valuation formula the author proposes

Rule of 40 > 50 **+** NRR > 120% **+** GRR > 90% **+** fintech > 30% of revenue **+** industry-specific AI → **8–10× ARR**.
