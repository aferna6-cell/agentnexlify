# 2026 SaaS Churn Rate Benchmarks: Data by Segment & Why Most Comparisons Mislead

**Published:** 05 Jul 2026 — 10 min read
**Author:** Shrestha Pratik

## Core Churn Benchmarks by Segment (2026)

| Segment | ACV Range | Typical Monthly Churn | Typical Annual Churn | Median NRR | Key Factor |
|---------|-----------|----------------------|----------------------|------------|------------|
| Enterprise | $50K+ | 0.3% - 0.7% | 4% - 8% | 110% - 125% | High switching costs |
| Mid-Market | $10K - $50K | 0.8% - 1.5% | 9% - 16% | 100% - 115% | Balancing scale & service |
| SMB | $1K - $10K | 2.5% - 5% | 25% - 45% | 90% - 100% | Low friction to switch |

**Sources cited:** Recurly 2025, Paddle Q2 2025, KeyBanc 2024 SaaS Survey, OpenView Partners Benchmarks

## Why Benchmark Comparisons Mislead

### Three Systematic Biases

**Survivorship Bias**
"Benchmark reports from sources like Bessemer, KeyBanc, and OpenView primarily survey companies that are still alive and growing."

**ACV Normalization Failure**
Aggregating dissimilar companies obscures meaning. "A 5% annual churn rate means something entirely different when you're losing five $200K contracts versus fifty $5K contracts."

**Contract Structure Distortion**
"A company with 100% annual contracts might show 0% churn for 11 months, then 8% churn in month 12."

## Key Metric Distinctions

### Logo vs. Revenue Churn
- **Logo Churn:** Percentage of customer accounts lost
- **Revenue Churn:** Percentage of MRR/ARR lost

The gap reveals whether you're losing wrong-fit or high-value accounts.

### Gross Revenue Retention (GRR) vs. Net Revenue Retention (NRR)

"NRR conflates expansion revenue with contraction revenue into a single net figure that hides the churn waterfall."

GRR strips expansion to show raw retention of existing revenue. "SaaS Capital's research shows a GRR below 85% is a red flag, regardless of NRR."

## Involuntary Churn Recovery

"Involuntary churn—cancellations caused by failed payments rather than customer decisions—accounts for 20-40% of total SaaS churn."

**Highest-impact recovery tactics:**
1. Smart Retry Logic
2. Pre-dunning Emails (7-10 days before expiration)
3. Card Updater Services
4. Grace Periods with in-app notifications

"Best-in-class save rates on failed payments exceed 70%. Companies without an optimized dunning process often recover less than 30%."

## Critical Insights

- "There is no single 'good' SaaS churn rate; benchmarks range from <5% annually for enterprise to over 30% for SMB-focused products."
- A $50K ACV enterprise SaaS with 4% annual churn is healthy; a $3K ACV SMB with 4% is exceptional. "The number is the same; the meaning is entirely different."
- "Median overall SaaS churn is 3.27% annually" (per Recurly 2025), but this excludes contraction MRR.
- Series B investors typically expect: "Annual net revenue churn below 10% and NRR above 100% for B2B SaaS."
- Churn stabilizes between "$10M and $20M ARR, when the customer base is large enough to smooth out individual account volatility."
