# The SaaS Retention Report: The AI Churn Wave

**Author:** Kyle Poyar, Analyst-in-Residence at ChartMogul

## Executive Summary

ARR is the foundation for SaaS metrics and valuation multiples — typically high-margin, predictable, growing revenue. AI startups disrupt this model: experimental AI products, contracts with high opt-out rates (70-80%), usage-based pricing, professional services. The industry faces an impending "gross retention apocalypse" as buyers scrutinize AI experiments and early adopters move to competing products.

## The AI Tourist Problem

Sample: ~2,700 B2B SaaS, 600 B2C SaaS, 200 AI-native (all $250k+ ARR).

**Retention Metrics (median):**
- B2B SaaS: 82% NRR (97% upper quartile)
- B2C SaaS: 49% NRR
- AI-native: 40% GRR, 48% NRR

AI-native retention improved through 2025: median GRR jumped from 27% (Jan) to 40% (Sept) — committed users remained, experimental adopters departed.

## Easy to Buy, Easy to Cancel

**By Monthly Price Point:**
- **>$250/mo**: 70% GRR, 85% NRR (comparable to B2B SaaS)
- **$50-$249/mo**: 45% GRR, 61% NRR (15 points worse than B2B)
- **<$50/mo**: 23% GRR, 32% NRR (20 points worse than B2B or B2C)

AI products differ via usage-based pricing tied to token costs. Forces early conversion, creates friction — users pay without fully understanding long-term value or workflow integration.

## Retention Equals Durable ARR

- Early-stage startups can hit 200%+ growth with NRR <40%, but face 3x risk of revenue contraction
- Companies reaching $5M ARR show substantially better gross/net retention than early-stage peers
- High-performing AI-native: 2x GRR and 2.5x NRR vs earlier-stage counterparts

Long-term viability depends on retention durability, not growth velocity alone.

## What to Do About Churn

### 1. Target Valuable Workflows
Move beyond consumer AI wrappers toward deeply embedded business solutions with greater differentiation from general-purpose LLMs.

### 2. Deliver Services Beyond AI
Forward-deployed engineers (FDEs) prototype/implement solutions for high-value customers, closing capability-to-value gap. "The FDE model trades margin for moat and tends to be worth it" long-term.

### 3. Avoid Overselling Initial Deals
Start small to prove value quickly, accelerate sales cycles, enable wider FDE deployment. Overpromising creates unrealistic expectations.

### 4. Narrow Product-to-Adoption Gaps
Customers struggle to keep pace with AI release velocity. Prioritize adoption acceleration to ensure feature understanding/implementation.

### 5. Sell Annual Plans
Annual plans correlate with 10-20 percentage point higher NRR than monthly. Tactics:
- Make annual the default option
- Request annual upgrades in months 2-3
- Offer substantial discounts (Grammarly cited 60%)
