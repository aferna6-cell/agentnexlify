# Competitor Landscape Snapshot — 2026-04-18

Pricing verified by direct scrape of each vendor's homepage + pricing page on 2026-04-18 via agent-browser CLI. Any amounts not shown on public pages are noted as "contact sales" rather than fabricated.

## GoHighLevel

- **Pricing (public tiers, monthly):**
  - Starter: **$97/mo** (or $970/yr) — 3 sub-accounts, unlimited contacts, unlimited users, all core features
  - Unlimited: **$297/mo** (or $2,970/yr) — unlimited sub-accounts, user/agent reporting, rebill phone+email no markup, basic API
  - Agency Pro (aka SaaS tier): **$497/mo** (or $4,970/yr) — SaaS Mode, automated sub-account creation, rebill with markup, advanced API
  - Enterprise: **contact sales** — dedicated CSM + account manager, dedicated Slack channel, custom dev, HIPAA, white-label mobile app included, QBRs, enterprise reporting
- **AI features:** Voice AI (call tracking + inbound), Conversation AI (chat widget), AI biz card scanner, AI for prospecting + ad manager, AI-assisted automations, AI review responses (implied via reputation tools). Homepage positions HL as "AI-powered business operating system."
- **Widget story:** Yes — "Chat Widget / Conversation AI" listed as a core capture tool. Widget is table-stakes, not the differentiator; GHL's hook is the all-in-one CRM/funnel/email/SMS/ad manager bundle.
- **Whitelabel:** Yes — SaaS Mode gated to Agency Pro ($497). White-label mobile app gated to Enterprise (contact sales).
- **Target customer:** Marketing agencies + "SaaSpreneurs" reselling HL to SMBs (explicit wording: "By marketers, for marketers").
- **Source URLs:**
  - https://www.gohighlevel.com/pricing
  - https://www.gohighlevel.com

## Drillbit

- **Pricing:** **$500/mo per location** base for a full-time AI employee ("Mason"). Usage-based billing kicks in after quota. Claims $100 of value guaranteed per $1 spent. No public tier ladder; single-SKU + usage.
- **AI features:** "Mason" AI employee handles phone calls, website inquiries, paid leads, ads — qualifies leads, nurtures via text/email/voice, books appointments against tech calendars, runs payment collections on unpaid invoices, mines past client data for upsell/re-engagement. Positioned as an employee replacement, not a tool.
- **Widget story:** Not widget-first. Website visits funnel in, but primary channels are phone + SMS + email. No explicit embeddable-widget positioning.
- **Whitelabel:** Not mentioned. Individual/franchisor "referral system" pays % of revenue — closer to affiliate than true white-label SaaS.
- **Target customer:** Home services + trades contractors + franchise networks ("ResiBrands" case study: $840K saved, 2.56x profit, +$5M new revenue in 90 days). Y Combinator-backed.
- **Source URLs:**
  - https://www.drillbit.com/ (drillbit.ai redirects to drillbit.com)

## Phonely

- **Pricing (public tiers, annual billing shown, monthly available):**
  - Free: **$0** — 100 min/mo (~50 calls), 100+ voices & languages, AI builder, email+SMS notifications, 1 free phone number
  - Starter: **$33/mo** (33% off annual) — 250 min/mo (~100 calls), premium voices, AI analytics, spam filtering, overage $0.35/min, 3 free phone numbers
  - Professional: **$99/mo** (33% off annual) — 750 min/mo (~300 calls), conversational AI SMS, advanced API + CRM integrations, overage $0.30/min, 5 free phone numbers, A/B testing
  - Enterprise: "as low as **5¢/min**" — contact sales, overage $0.25/min, full agent buildout, fine tuning, SIP trunking, HIPAA BAA, dedicated account manager
- **AI features:** Voice AI answering, AI webchat (same flows as voice), AI SMS, 1,000+ voices & voice cloning, 100+ languages, transcripts + AI analytics, A/B testing, call transfer & routing, phone payments, custom knowledgebase (website import, scales with tier).
- **Widget story:** Yes — AI webchat brings the same Phonely flows to website chat. But voice (phone) is the primary positioning, webchat is a secondary channel.
- **Whitelabel:** Not publicly offered on pricing page. Enterprise tier has branded caller ID. No mention of reseller/SaaS-mode white-labeling.
- **Target customer:** Call-center operators, healthcare, SMBs, insurance, home services, legal, real estate, hospitality — positioned as end customer, not agency reseller. Just raised $16M Series A (Base10 Partners, YC, enterprise co-investors).
- **Source URLs:**
  - https://www.phonely.ai
  - https://www.phonely.ai/pricing

## Birdeye

- **Pricing:** **Hidden.** Page has 3 named tiers (Starter, Growth, Dominate) with placeholder "$ /location/month" but NO dollar amounts. Visitors must fill out a "Pricing Configurator" form (business details + consent to communications) to receive customized pricing. Effectively contact-sales.
- **AI features:** Reviews AI (in all tiers), Listings AI (all tiers), Messaging AI (all tiers), Social AI (Growth + Dominate), Chatbot AI (Dominate only).
- **Widget story:** "Messaging AI" and "Chatbot AI" exist but are gated — Chatbot AI only in the top "Dominate" tier. Widget is not the primary hook; reviews + local-search dominance is.
- **Whitelabel:** Not offered on the public pricing page. There is a "Do you want to be a reseller or partner?" contact form link — implies partner program exists but details are sales-gated.
- **Target customer:** Multi-location local businesses priced per-location. Reviews/reputation management is the core wedge.
- **Source URLs:**
  - https://birdeye.com/pricing/

## Podium

- **Pricing (public tiers, monthly):**
  - Core: **$399/mo** — lead conversion platform, AI review responses, basic automations/reporting, 200+ integrations, mobile app, CSM, 250 bulk-message credits, 5 phone numbers
  - Pro: **$599/mo** (marked "Best Value") — everything in Core plus AI Concierge, AI Reputation Specialist, AI phone call summaries, automatic lead routing, advanced automations, lead capture forms, surveys, 500 bulk credits, 15 phone numbers
  - Signature: **custom pricing** (contact sales) — adds AI Scheduler, AI Salesperson, AI Marketer, multi-location, custom billing, priority support, QBRs
  - HVAC vertical: **contact sales for applicable pricing** (noted on every tier)
  - Add-on: Podium Phones seats $30/user (1-4 users), $25/user (5+), $500 network optimization fee per location
  - Add-on: "AI Employee" ("Jerry") — pricing not shown on pricing page, marketed as addon to any plan
- **AI features:** AI suggested replies, AI review responses, AI Concierge, AI Reputation Specialist, AI phone call summaries, AI text marketing generator, AI Instant Answers (addon), AI Scheduler/Salesperson/Marketer (Signature only), AI Employee "Jerry" (addon, all tiers).
- **Widget story:** **Yes and strong.** "Website Chat Widget" listed as core feature across all tiers. "Remove Podium Branding" + "Advanced Website Widget Branding" gated to Pro+. One case study cites 1,500+ webchat leads/month from a single customer.
- **Whitelabel:** Partial — "Remove Podium Branding" gated above Core. No agency/reseller SaaS mode like GHL. Partner program exists ("Become a Partner" + "Find a Solution Partner" links) but terms are sales-gated.
- **Target customer:** 100K+ local businesses; verticals called out explicitly: automotive, auto body, med spa, dental, HVAC, plumbing, jewelry, furniture, appliance, mattress, large biz.
- **Source URLs:**
  - https://www.podium.com/pricing

1. **Price floor below GHL.** GHL Starter is $97/mo with 3 sub-accounts and a widget buried in a feature list. AgentNexLiFy's `growth` at $249 is above that entry but undercuts Podium Core ($399) and Birdeye (hidden but analyst consensus $300-600). 2. **Widget-first, not widget-as-one-of-twelve.** Every competitor lists chat widget as a sub-bullet of a mega-platform. Podium's widget is real but gated behind a $399 CRM. Phonely's webchat is a bolt-on to voice. Birdeye gates chatbot AI to the top tier only. AgentNexLiFy can position the widget as the whole product — embed in 30 seconds, no CRM migration, no account manager call, no 14-day trial funnel.

Drillbit pitches learning from past client data but that's internal. AgentNexLiFy's vertical KB-per-tenant pattern (per CLAUDE.md positioning) is a clean differentiator if it's surfaced in marketing.

5. **Vertical positioning under the agency layer.** GHL sells to agencies who resell to SMBs. Drillbit owns trades. Podium is spread across 10 verticals. There's open ground for a "widget that sells itself to the end business, skipping the agency middleman" — especially for verticals Drillbit doesn't cover (legal, dental, med-spa, tutoring, fitness). AgentNexLiFy can stack vertical KBs faster than GHL can retrofit its agency ladder.
