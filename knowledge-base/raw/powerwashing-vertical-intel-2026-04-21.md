## Service Categories

- **House wash** — full exterior siding rinse. Soft-wash (low PSI + surfactant for vinyl, stucco, painted wood) vs high-pressure (brick, concrete block).
- **Driveway / sidewalk** — concrete + paver flatwork, surface-cleaner attachment, oil-stain treatment add-on.
- **Deck / patio** — wood softwash with brightener + sealer upsell; composite gets gentler mix.
- **Fence** — wood or vinyl, linear-foot pricing, stain prep a common upsell.
- **Roof** — soft-wash ONLY (sodium hypochlorite mix); high pressure voids shingle warranties.
- **Gutter cleaning** — exterior brightening + interior debris removal; often bundled with house wash.
- **Commercial storefront** — sidewalk gum removal, dumpster-pad degrease, recurring contracts.
- **Fleet wash** — on-site truck/trailer rinse, per-unit pricing, night-shift friendly.

## Pricing Benchmarks (industry-typical ranges)

- House wash: $250–$650 flat by sq ft (1–2 story, up to ~3000 sq ft).
- Driveway: $0.15–$0.35/sq ft, or $150–$400 flat (2-car).
- Deck/patio: $0.30–$0.60/sq ft, seal add-on $1–$2/sq ft.
- Fence: $1.50–$3.50/linear ft.
- Roof soft-wash: $0.20–$0.60/sq ft, typical $450–$900.
- Gutter cleaning: $100–$250 single-story, $200–$400 two-story.
- Commercial storefront: $75–$200/visit recurring, $0.10–$0.25/sq ft one-off.
- Fleet wash: $15–$40 per vehicle volume discount.

All ranges regional; tenant overrides required in KB.

## Top Objections + Response Patterns

- **"Why so expensive?"** — itemize labor, chemicals, insurance, equipment; offer package discount; anchor to damage cost if DIY.
- **"Will it damage siding/paint?"** — emphasize soft-wash tech, insured, manufacturer-approved PSI, before/after photos.
- **"Can you come this weekend?"** — offer next 2–3 slots; waitlist if booked.
- **"What if it rains?"** — free reschedule policy, rain actually helps rinse.
- **"Are you licensed and insured?"** — yes, $1M–$2M liability, COI on request.
- **"Safe for my dog/plants/kids?"** — biodegradable detergents, pre-rinse landscaping, pet-safe after dry.
- **"Pay before or after?"** — after completion, walkthrough approval, card/ACH/check.
- **"What if I'm not happy?"** — 48-hour touchup guarantee, free revisit.

## Booking Flow — Required Fields

1. Service address / zip
2. Approx home sq ft or stories
3. Specific surfaces (checkboxes: house, driveway, deck, roof, fence, gutters)
4. Last-washed timeframe (never / <1yr / 1–3yr / 3+yr)
5. Preferred date window (this week / next 2 weeks / flex)
6. Contact (name + phone + email)

## Seasonal Demand

- **Peak 1**: March–May (spring cleanup, pollen, pre-listing real estate).
- **Peak 2**: Sept–Oct (fall prep, pre-holiday house wash).
- **Bumps**: pre-July 4 deck refresh, pre-Thanksgiving gutter clean.
- **Trough**: Dec–Feb (freeze regions); warmer markets see steady commercial work.
- Booked 1–3 weeks out at peak; same-week in trough.

## Small-Operator Differentiators

- Local ownership, owner answers phone/text.
- Same-week or next-day scheduling vs chain 2–3 week waits.
- Soft-wash specialty (chains often default to damaging high pressure).
- Neighborhood referral discounts, Nextdoor/Facebook group presence.
- Bundled pricing flexibility; no upsell scripts.

## Bad-Fit Red Flags

- Solo operator, phone-only, no website or Google Business Profile.
- "Hates computers" — won't check dashboard or follow up on widget leads.
- No email/SMS on file for customers; all word-of-mouth.
- Under 20 jobs/month — widget ROI doesn't clear $249/mo.
- Season-only side hustle with no winter revenue.
- Unwilling to let AI answer price questions.

## Sales Pitch Angles

1. **After-hours capture** — operator is on a roof 8am–6pm; widget books the 7pm Facebook-ad-clicker instead of losing them.
2. **Instant quote request → callback next morning** — bot captures 6 fields, SMS pings operator, response within 12h beats competitor 48h.
3. **FAQ deflection** — widget answers "licensed?", "safe for dog?", "weather policy?" saving 10–15 phone calls/week; operator calls only hot leads.
