# Phonely — Voice-First AI with Webchat Bolt-On (2026-04)

Phonely (`phonely.ai`) is a voice-first AI vendor with a transparent four-tier pricing ladder as of 2026-04-18. Free tier gives 100 minutes per month (~50 calls), 100+ voices and languages, an AI builder, email+SMS notifications, and one free phone number. Starter at $33/mo (33% off annual) raises to 250 minutes (~100 calls), premium voices, AI analytics, spam filtering, and three phone numbers; overage is $0.35/min. Professional at $99/mo (33% off annual) steps to 750 minutes (~300 calls), conversational AI SMS, advanced API and CRM integrations, A/B testing, five phone numbers, and $0.30/min overage. Enterprise is contact-sales with "as low as 5¢/min," a BAA for HIPAA, SIP trunking, dedicated account manager, full agent buildout, and fine-tuning.

The product surface covers voice answering, AI webchat reusing the same flows as voice, AI SMS, 1,000+ voices plus voice cloning, 100+ languages, transcripts, analytics, A/B testing, call transfer and routing, phone-based payments, and a custom knowledge base imported from a customer's website. Importantly, webchat is framed as bringing voice flows to the web, not as the primary product — voice is the lead. White-label is not publicly offered; Enterprise gets branded caller ID only. No reseller SaaS mode on the pricing page.

Phonely's target customer is the end business — call-center operators, healthcare, insurance, home services, legal, real estate, hospitality — not an agency reseller. Funding signal: a $16M Series A led by Base10 Partners with Y Combinator and enterprise co-investors. Voice-first with webchat-second is distinct from [[podium]] (widget-strong) and [[gohighlevel]] (agency-reseller). See [[competitive-landscape-march-2026]] for the positioning matrix.

## Key Concepts

- **Minutes-based tiers** — Free 100, Starter 250, Professional 750, Enterprise contract. Overage drops from $0.35 → $0.30 → $0.25/min by tier.
- **Webchat as bolt-on** — Same flows as voice; not pitched as the primary product.
- **BAA + HIPAA** — Enterprise-only, contact-sales.
- **No white-label** — Branded caller ID on Enterprise; no reseller program published.

## Relevance to AgentNexLiFy

Phonely prices voice by minutes, which makes direct comparison to AgentNexLiFy's flat plans (`chatbot` $19.99 · `agent_os` $99.99; pricing updated 2026-06-15) awkward for the buyer — a usage model versus a subscription model. That is a wedge: SMBs historically prefer flat pricing, especially when their call volume is unpredictable. The webchat-as-bolt-on posture is the clearest opening: AgentNexLiFy's widget-first narrative lands hardest against a vendor whose widget is explicitly secondary. Do not underestimate Phonely's Free tier — 100 minutes/mo and a free number is a low-friction top-of-funnel that AgentNexLiFy should match conceptually (widget embed in 30 seconds, no credit card, generous ceiling) even without voice minutes.
