# GoHighLevel — Public Pricing and Widget Positioning (2026-04)

GoHighLevel is the AgentNexLiFy incumbent comparison. As of 2026-04-18, its public pricing ladder is Starter $97/mo ($970/yr), Unlimited $297/mo ($2,970/yr), Agency Pro $497/mo ($4,970/yr), and an Enterprise tier that is contact-sales only. Each tier ships unlimited contacts and users; the climb between tiers is about sub-accounts, rebilling, API access, and white-label. Starter grants 3 sub-accounts, Unlimited removes the cap, Agency Pro unlocks SaaS Mode (automated sub-account provisioning) and rebill-with-markup, and Enterprise adds dedicated CSM, white-label mobile app, HIPAA, QBRs, and dedicated dev support.

GoHighLevel's AI surface now covers Voice AI for inbound calls, Conversation AI for the chat widget, AI biz-card scanning, AI-assisted automations, AI for prospecting + ad manager, and review-response AI. The homepage frames the product as "the AI-powered business operating system," not a chat widget. That framing matters: in their narrative, the widget is one of twelve capture tools, not the product itself.

White-label is the distribution moat. SaaS Mode at $497/mo lets agencies automate spinning up new sub-accounts under their own brand; white-label mobile apps are Enterprise-only. The explicit wording — "By marketers, for marketers" — confirms the target is marketing agencies reselling to SMBs, not end-business buyers. That leaves a clean niche for direct-to-end-business widget positioning that skips the agency middleman. See [[competitive-landscape-march-2026]] for the broader matrix and [[gohighlevel-agency-platform]] for the earlier scale-metrics writeup that this profile extends.

## Key Concepts

- **SaaS Mode** — Agency Pro+ feature that provisions sub-accounts automatically and lets the agency rebill GHL usage with markup. Core to the white-label wedge.
- **Sub-account** — One end-client tenant inside a GHL agency. Starter caps at 3; Unlimited+ removes the cap.
- **Conversation AI** — GHL's chat widget + AI reply layer. Listed alongside Voice AI and SMS automation, not pitched as standalone.
- **Agency Pro vs Enterprise** — Agency Pro ($497) unlocks SaaS Mode and public APIs; Enterprise adds HIPAA, dedicated CSM, white-label mobile, and QBRs — priced via sales.

## Relevance to AgentNexLiFy

GoHighLevel's Starter at $97/mo is below AgentNexLiFy's `growth` plan ($249/mo), so the pitch is not "cheapest." It is "cheapest widget-first." GHL buyers at Starter pay for a CRM bundle most of them do not fully use; the widget is buried. AgentNexLiFy wins by positioning the widget as the whole product — 30-second embed, no CRM migration, no agency call, no 14-day funnel. Voice AI parity is now table-stakes; shipping voice alongside widget closes the obvious comparison. The vertical knowledge-base-per-tenant pattern is the differentiator GHL cannot retrofit without rewriting its agency ladder.
