# Drillbit — YC-Backed AI Employee for Trades Contractors

Drillbit (domain `drillbit.com`, with `drillbit.ai` redirecting in) is a Y Combinator-backed vertical competitor focused squarely on home-services and trades contractors. Its product is branded as "Mason," an AI employee rather than a tool, priced at $500/mo per location base with usage-based billing after quota. There is no public tier ladder — the SKU is single, and the company makes a "$100 of value per $1 spent" guarantee on the pricing page. On 2026-04-18 the public page showed no discounting, no monthly/annual split, and no free tier.

Mason handles inbound phone calls, website inquiries, paid leads, and ad traffic; qualifies leads; nurtures via text, email, and voice; books against tech calendars; collects on unpaid invoices; and mines past client data for upsell and re-engagement. Channels are phone, SMS, and email. There is no embeddable widget positioning on the public marketing — website visits funnel in, but the primary surface is voice. A featured ResiBrands case study claims $840K saved, 2.56x profit, and $5M new revenue in 90 days.

White-label is absent. A referral program pays individuals and franchisors a percentage of revenue, which is closer to affiliate than true white-label SaaS. The target is trades contractors and franchise networks — not marketing agencies, unlike [[gohighlevel]]. That vertical focus is a wedge that AgentNexLiFy does not compete with directly in home-services trades, but leaves open in legal, dental, med-spa, tutoring, and fitness. See [[competitive-landscape-march-2026]] for positioning context.

## Key Concepts

- **Mason** — Drillbit's branded AI employee. Single-SKU product, not a tier.
- **$500/mo per location** — base price; usage-based billing kicks in after quota.
- **Referral, not white-label** — affiliates earn a revenue cut; no reseller SaaS mode.
- **Vertical focus** — home services and trades contractors; franchise networks secondary.

## Relevance to AgentNexLiFy

Drillbit owns trades contractors via voice + employee framing. AgentNexLiFy is not trying to out-Drillbit in home services — the clean move is to stack vertical knowledge bases in categories Drillbit does not touch (legal, dental, med-spa, tutoring, fitness) and lead with a widget-first narrative. Price is also a signal: Drillbit's $500/mo per location base sits above AgentNexLiFy's `professional` at $499/mo, which gives room to undercut Drillbit in adjacent verticals with a widget + vertical KB pitch. Copy what Drillbit does well: single-SKU simplicity and outcome-based marketing ("$100 value per $1"). Avoid what they skip: widget-first distribution.
