# Podium — Widget-Strong Lead Conversion Platform (2026-04)

Podium is the closest competitor to AgentNexLiFy on widget strength. As of 2026-04-18 its public pricing ladder is Core $399/mo, Pro $599/mo (marked "Best Value"), and Signature at custom pricing. Core ships lead conversion, AI review responses, basic automations and reporting, 200+ integrations, mobile app, CSM, 250 bulk-message credits, and 5 phone numbers. Pro adds AI Concierge, AI Reputation Specialist, AI phone-call summaries, automatic lead routing, advanced automations, lead capture forms, surveys, 500 bulk credits, and 15 phone numbers. Signature adds AI Scheduler, AI Salesperson, AI Marketer, multi-location, custom billing, priority support, and QBRs. HVAC pricing is contact-sales on every tier. Podium Phones add-on seats are $30/user (1-4) or $25/user (5+), with a one-time $500 network optimization fee per location. An AI Employee named "Jerry" is marketed as an addon to any plan, pricing not shown.

The AI surface is broad: AI suggested replies, review responses, Concierge, Reputation Specialist, call summaries, text-marketing generator, Instant Answers addon, and Signature-only Scheduler/Salesperson/Marketer plus the Jerry addon. Widget story is strong and explicit — "Website Chat Widget" is listed as a core feature across all tiers, and a case study cites 1,500+ webchat leads/month for a single customer. "Remove Podium Branding" and "Advanced Website Widget Branding" gate above Core, so white-label-lite kicks in at Pro+. No full reseller SaaS mode like [[gohighlevel]]. Target customer: 100K+ local businesses across automotive, auto body, med spa, dental, HVAC, plumbing, jewelry, furniture, appliance, mattress, and "large biz."

See [[competitive-landscape-march-2026]] for positioning against Birdeye, Drillbit, Phonely, GHL.

## Key Concepts

- **Website Chat Widget** — listed as core feature on every tier; the strongest widget story among these five competitors.
- **Podium Phones** — per-seat add-on with a $500 per-location optimization fee.
- **Jerry (AI Employee)** — addon product with pricing not disclosed on pricing page.
- **Branding removal** — Pro+; partial white-label, not full reseller mode.

## Relevance to AgentNexLiFy

Podium is the hardest direct competitor because its widget is real, priced on page, and ships on every tier. The fight is not "do they have a widget" — they do. The fight is friction and price. Podium Core at $399/mo is above AgentNexLiFy's `growth` at $249/mo and pulls buyers into a CRM ecosystem they may not want. AgentNexLiFy wins on: (1) lower entry price with widget parity, (2) no CRM migration required, (3) per-tenant vertical knowledge base — Podium has 10+ verticals but no per-customer KB story, (4) 30-second embed narrative against Podium's CSM-onboarded sales motion. Do not try to outbuild Podium's 200+ integrations library. Compete on embed friction + price + vertical KB.
