# Birdeye — Reviews-First Platform with Hidden Pricing (2026-04)

Birdeye is the reviews-and-reputation incumbent for multi-location local businesses. As of 2026-04-18 its public pricing page listed three tiers — Starter, Growth, and Dominate — with placeholder "$ /location/month" text and zero dollar amounts. To see a number, prospects fill out a Pricing Configurator asking for business details and opt-in to communications. This is effectively contact-sales dressed as a pricing page. Analyst consensus sits in the $300-600/mo range depending on location count and tier.

The AI surface is tier-gated: Reviews AI, Listings AI, and Messaging AI ship on all tiers; Social AI starts at Growth; Chatbot AI is Dominate-only. The chatbot is therefore the top-tier feature, not the wedge. The core selling motion is reviews volume and local-search dominance, not widget-first capture. White-label is not offered on the public page, though a "reseller or partner?" link hints at a partner program with sales-gated terms. Per-location pricing is the model, which fits multi-location retail, auto, healthcare, dental groups.

Birdeye vs AgentNexLiFy is a different shape of competition than [[gohighlevel]] (widget buried in CRM) or [[podium]] (widget-strong). Birdeye's widget is optional and top-tier only; the company sells reviews and listings first. For widget-first positioning, Birdeye is the easiest wedge — the buyer who wants a chat widget has to climb to Dominate, navigate a sales call, and accept hidden pricing. See [[competitive-landscape-march-2026]].

## Key Concepts

- **Pricing Configurator** — form that gates all dollar amounts behind lead capture.
- **Tiered AI unlocks** — Reviews/Listings/Messaging everywhere; Social on Growth+; Chatbot only on Dominate.
- **Per-location pricing** — designed for multi-location chains; punishes small single-location SMBs.
- **No public white-label** — partner program exists but terms are sales-gated.
