Legal is a high-intent vertical for AgentNexLiFy because a website visitor with a legal problem is usually stressed, ready to act, and comparing two or three firms in the same hour. The questions below are the ones potential clients actually type into a chat widget on a law firm site: do you offer free consultations, what kind of cases do you handle, how much do you charge, do you take cases on contingency, how do I talk to a lawyer. A firm that answers these fast and books the consultation captures the client before they call the next firm on the list. The answers are written generic so they apply across any law firm tenant, whether the practice is personal injury, family, criminal, estate, or business law, and a specific firm overrides the practice areas, fee structure, and policies with its own values during onboarding. The content here is what the full-text search indexes, so each question is phrased the way a potential client phrases it and each answer is short enough to send as a chat reply.

The defining constraint for this vertical is that the bot does not give legal advice. A visitor describing the facts of their case wants to know "do I have a case" or "what should I do," and the correct move is always to capture the details and route them to an attorney consultation, never to answer the legal question. The fee answers use ranges and "depends on the case" language on purpose, because legal fees vary by practice area, complexity, hourly rate, and whether the matter is flat-fee, hourly, or contingency. Confidentiality matters more here than in most verticals, so the intake answers make clear that details shared are kept private and reviewed by the firm. The goal of every answer is the same: move a qualified visitor toward a consultation booking while collecting enough context for the attorney to prepare.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do you offer a free consultation?**
Many firms offer a free initial consultation to review your situation and explain your options, though some charge a flat fee for the first meeting depending on the practice area. The consultation is where an attorney looks at your specific facts. We can check the policy for this firm and book a time for you now.

**What types of cases or practice areas do you handle?**
Firms focus on specific areas such as personal injury, family law, criminal defense, estate planning, business law, immigration, or real estate. Tell us briefly what your situation involves and we can confirm whether this firm handles it, and if not, point you in the right direction. We can also book a consultation to go over the details.

**How much do you charge? What are your fees?**
Legal fees depend on the type of case and how it is billed. Some matters are a flat fee, some are billed hourly, and some like many injury cases are taken on contingency. A specific quote comes after an attorney reviews your situation. We can set up a consultation where the fee structure is explained for your case.

**Do you take cases on contingency?**
Many firms handle certain case types, often personal injury or some employment matters, on a contingency basis, which means the fee is a percentage of the recovery and you generally pay nothing up front. Whether contingency applies depends on the case type and the facts. We can confirm at a consultation and book one for you.

**Do I have a case? What are my chances?**
That is exactly what an attorney decides after reviewing your specific facts, so it is not something we can answer here. We do not give legal advice over chat. The best step is to share a few details so an attorney can review your situation at a consultation, and we can schedule that now.

**How do I schedule a consultation?**
You can book right here in the chat, on the website booking page, or by phone during business hours. To book, you pick a time and give a short description of your matter so the attorney is prepared. You get a confirmation by text or email.

**What should I bring or prepare for the consultation?**
It helps to bring any documents related to your matter, such as contracts, letters, court papers, police reports, medical records, or correspondence, along with a timeline of what happened. You do not need everything organized perfectly. The attorney will tell you what else is needed after the first meeting.

**Is what I share kept confidential?**
The details you share with the firm are kept private and reviewed by the firm's team. Information you provide to schedule a consultation is used to help the attorney prepare. An attorney can explain how confidentiality and privilege apply to your specific situation at the consultation.

**How long do I have to file? Is there a deadline?**
Legal deadlines exist and they vary a lot by case type and location, so the firm cannot confirm yours over chat. Because some deadlines are strict, it is worth speaking with an attorney soon rather than waiting. We can book a consultation quickly so the firm can review the timing for your matter.

**What are your hours? Are you available after hours or on weekends?**
Most firms keep weekday business hours, and many take new-client requests after hours through the website or chat so nothing is missed. Even when the office is closed, you can leave your details here and the firm follows up the next business day. We can capture your matter now.

**Do you offer payment plans?**
Some firms offer payment plans or flexible arrangements depending on the case type and fee structure. Contingency cases generally do not require payment up front. We can note your question and an attorney can go over the options at the consultation.

**Can you handle my case if I'm in a different city or state?**
It depends on where the firm is licensed and where your matter is based, since attorneys are admitted in specific jurisdictions. Tell us your location and the type of case and we can check whether this firm can help or refer you on. A consultation can confirm the details.

**What happens after I contact you?**
After you reach out, the firm reviews the details you share and an attorney or intake team follows up to schedule or confirm your consultation. At that meeting the attorney reviews your situation, explains your options, and goes over fees. We can start that process by booking a time now.

**I already have a lawyer. Can I switch or get a second opinion?**
Many firms will meet with you for a second opinion or to discuss taking over a matter, though there are things to consider when changing representation mid-case. An attorney can walk you through that at a consultation. We can schedule one and note that you have current representation.

**How do I reach an attorney directly?**
The fastest way is to book a consultation, where you speak with an attorney about your matter. For quick questions, you can leave your details here and the firm follows up. We do not answer legal questions in chat, but we make sure the right person reaches you.

## Key Concepts

- **No legal advice in chat**: The bot never answers the legal question itself. It captures the facts and routes the visitor to an attorney consultation, which keeps the firm safe and still wins the client.
- **Consultation-first flow**: The conversion event for this vertical is a booked consultation, so nearly every answer ends by offering to schedule one.
- **Fee structure language**: Legal fees are flat, hourly, or contingency depending on case type, so answers use ranges and "depends on the case" and confirm at consultation.
- **Practice-area routing**: Visitors describe a situation, and the AI front desk confirms whether the firm handles that area and offers a referral path when it does not.
- **Confidentiality and intake**: Details shared are kept private and used to prepare the attorney, with privilege questions answered by the attorney.
- **Deadline sensitivity**: Legal deadlines vary and can be strict, so the answer creates urgency toward a consultation without stating any specific deadline as fact.
