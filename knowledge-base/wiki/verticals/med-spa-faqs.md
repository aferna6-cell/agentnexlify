Pricing for aesthetics work is unit-based or area-based, not a single flat number, so the answers use ranges and "priced per unit or per area" language on purpose. Botox is sold by the unit, filler by the syringe, and laser by the area and number of sessions, which means a fixed price would be wrong for most visitors. Downtime and safety questions come up often for this vertical and matter more than they do for a salon, so they get direct, plain answers that still send the visitor to a provider for anything medical.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How much is Botox?**
Botox is usually priced per unit, and the number of units depends on the area treated and the result you want. A small area like frown lines uses fewer units than treating the forehead and eyes together. Most clinics quote a per-unit price and confirm the total at your consultation once a provider sees the area. We can book that consultation now.

**How much does filler cost?**
Dermal filler is usually priced per syringe, and how many syringes you need depends on the area, like lips, cheeks, or under the eyes. Lips often take one syringe and cheeks can take more. A provider confirms the plan and price at your consultation. We can give a starting range and set that up.

**Do I need a consultation before treatment?**
Most aesthetic treatments start with a consultation so a provider can review your goals, your medical history, and what is realistic for you. Some clinics do the consultation and the treatment in the same visit, and others split them. We can book a consultation and note what you are interested in.

**What treatments do you offer?**
Med spas commonly offer injectables like Botox and filler, laser treatments, chemical peels, facials, microneedling, and skin tightening. Many also do hair removal and body treatments. Tell us what concern you want to address and we can confirm which treatments fit and book a consultation.

**How long does Botox or filler last?**
Botox results commonly last around three to four months, and filler often lasts longer, from several months to over a year depending on the product and the area. Results vary by person and by how your body breaks down the product. A provider can give you a closer estimate for your treatment.

**Is it safe? Does it hurt?**
These treatments are common and are performed by trained providers, and most clients describe the discomfort as mild. Many treatments use a numbing cream or a fine needle to keep it comfortable. A provider reviews your health history first and answers safety questions specific to you at the consultation.

**How much downtime will I need?**
Downtime depends on the treatment. Injectables often have little to no downtime, with possible minor swelling or bruising for a few days. Lasers and peels can need more recovery, from a day to a week, depending on the strength. We can tell you the typical downtime for the treatment you are considering.

**Can I get a treatment today or this week?**
Same-day or same-week openings depend on the treatment and whether you need a consultation first. Some clinics do consult and treat in one visit, and others book the treatment after. Tell us what you want and your preferred time and we can show you the soonest opening.

**How do I book an appointment or consultation?**
You can book right here in the chat, on the website booking page, or by phone during business hours. To book, you pick the treatment or a consultation and an open time, and you get a confirmation by text or email. We can note your main concern so the provider is prepared.

**Do you offer financing or payment plans?**
Many med spas offer financing through third-party providers or in-house plans, and some run package pricing that lowers the per-treatment cost. The options depend on the clinic. Ask us and we can tell you what is available and book a consultation to go over it.

**Am I a good candidate? Can I get this done if I'm pregnant or have a condition?**
A provider decides candidacy after reviewing your health history, and some treatments are not recommended during pregnancy, breastfeeding, or with certain conditions or medications. Share any concerns and we will pass them to the provider, who confirms what is safe for you at the consultation.

**What is your cancellation or no-show policy?**
Most clinics ask for notice before your appointment, often 24 to 48 hours, and some charge a fee for late cancellations or no-shows on booked provider time. The exact window and fee depend on the clinic. We can confirm the policy for your appointment.

**Do you take memberships or sell packages?**
Many med spas offer memberships or treatment packages that bundle sessions at a lower price, common for things like laser hair removal or regular facials. The structure depends on the clinic. Ask us and we can explain the current options.

**How should I prepare before my appointment?**
Preparation depends on the treatment. For injectables, many clinics suggest avoiding alcohol and certain supplements that thin the blood for a day or two to reduce bruising. For laser, avoiding sun and tanning beforehand is common. We will send the specific prep steps for your treatment when you book.

**Who performs the treatments?**
Treatments are performed by qualified providers such as nurse injectors, nurse practitioners, physician assistants, or physicians, depending on the treatment and local rules. A provider oversees your care and reviews your history. We can tell you who would handle your treatment at this clinic.

**What forms of payment do you take?**
Most med spas take major cards and many take digital payments, plus financing for larger treatments. Some also accept HSA or FSA for eligible services. Check the specific clinic for its accepted methods, and we can confirm when you book.

## Key Concepts

- **Consultation-first flow**: Most aesthetic treatments start with a provider consultation, so the AI front desk routes high-consideration visitors to a consultation booking rather than a flat price.
- **Unit and area pricing**: Botox is per unit, filler is per syringe, laser is per area and session count, so answers use ranges and confirm totals at consultation.
- **Downtime expectations**: Recovery ranges from none for injectables to a week for stronger lasers and peels, and visitors ask about it before booking.
- **Candidacy and screening**: Pregnancy, conditions, and medications affect what is safe, so the AI front desk collects concerns and routes anything medical to the provider.
- **Financing and packages**: Larger treatments often use financing, memberships, or packages, which the AI front desk can surface to move the visitor toward a consultation.
- **Provider type**: Treatments are done by qualified injectors and providers, and visitors sometimes ask who performs the work.
