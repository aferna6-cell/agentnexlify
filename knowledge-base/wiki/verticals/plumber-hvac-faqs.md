## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do you handle emergencies or after-hours calls?**
Many plumbing and HVAC companies offer emergency or after-hours service for urgent problems like burst pipes, major leaks, no heat in winter, or no cooling in extreme heat. If your problem is urgent, tell us what is happening and we will get you to the fastest available help. If the location does not run 24/7, we book you for the earliest next opening.

**I have a burst pipe or active flooding. What do I do right now?**
If water is actively flooding, shut off your main water valve if you can reach it safely, then turn off electricity to any wet area if it is safe. This is an emergency, so let us get your address and we will dispatch the soonest available technician or get you to the on-call line.

**I smell gas. What should I do?**
A gas smell is a safety emergency. Leave the building, do not flip switches or use any flame, and call your gas utility or emergency services from outside. Once you are safe, we can schedule a technician to inspect and repair the source.

**My heat or air conditioning stopped working. Can someone come today?**
No heat in winter or no cooling in a heat wave is treated as urgent by most companies. Tell us your address and the symptoms and we will show you the soonest available visit, often same day depending on demand and your area.

**What are your hours?**
Most home-services companies run standard daytime hours on weekdays, with some Saturday availability and emergency coverage outside those hours. Routine work is scheduled during business hours, while urgent problems may be handled after hours. Check the specific location for exact hours and emergency coverage.

**What areas do you serve?**
Companies usually cover a set of nearby towns or a radius around their base. Tell us your city or zip code and we can confirm whether you are in the service area and book a visit. If you are just outside it, we will let you know any travel consideration.

**How much does a service call cost?**
Most companies charge a service-call or diagnostic fee to send a technician out and assess the problem. That fee is often applied toward the repair if you move forward. Repair pricing depends on the job, so the technician gives a quote on site after seeing the issue. Tell us the problem and we can give a rough idea.

**Do you offer free estimates?**
Many companies give free estimates for larger jobs like a new water heater, a furnace or AC replacement, or a repipe, while smaller diagnostic visits may carry a fee. Whether an estimate is free depends on the job type and the location. Tell us what you need and we can confirm.

**How much is a new water heater, furnace, or AC unit?**
Replacement pricing varies widely by unit size, efficiency, fuel type, and the work needed to install. These jobs usually get a written estimate after a technician sees your setup. We can schedule a no-pressure estimate so you get a real number for your home.

**How soon can you come out?**
Timing depends on whether the job is urgent and how booked the schedule is. Emergencies get priority and are often same day. Routine work is usually within a few days. Give us your address and the problem and we will show you the soonest opening.

**Are your technicians licensed and insured?**
Reputable plumbing and HVAC companies use licensed and insured technicians for the work that requires it. The specific licenses depend on the trade and the area. We can confirm the credentials for the location handling your job.

**Do you charge by the hour or by the job?**
Pricing models vary. Some companies use flat-rate pricing per job so you know the cost up front, while others bill by the hour plus parts. Either way, you get a quote before the work starts. Tell us the job and we can explain how that location prices it.

**Do you offer financing on large repairs or replacements?**
Many companies offer financing for big-ticket work like a system replacement or a repipe. Terms and approval depend on the provider. We can note that you are interested and the team can walk you through the options.

**Do you give a warranty or guarantee on your work?**
Most companies back their labor with a workmanship guarantee, and new equipment usually carries a manufacturer warranty. The exact terms depend on the job and the parts. We can confirm what applies to your repair or install.

**Can you fix a leak, clogged drain, or running toilet?**
Yes, those are common plumbing jobs. A technician diagnoses the cause and quotes the repair on site, since a simple clog and a deeper line problem are priced differently. Tell us what you are seeing and we can book a visit.

**Do you do drain cleaning or sewer line work?**
Many plumbing companies handle drain cleaning, camera inspections, and sewer line repair. These range from a quick snake to a larger excavation depending on the problem. We can schedule a technician to assess and quote it.

**Do you do maintenance plans or tune-ups?**
Many HVAC and plumbing companies offer maintenance plans or seasonal tune-ups that keep equipment running and may include priority scheduling and discounts. Plan details vary by location. We can tell you what is offered and sign you up.

**What should I do to get ready for the visit?**
Clear access to the area that needs work, like the space around your water heater, furnace, or the affected fixture. Keep pets secured and note any details you have noticed, such as sounds, smells, or when the problem started. That helps the technician work faster.

**What payment methods do you accept?**
Most companies take cards and many take digital payments, plus cash or check. Larger jobs may offer financing. Check the specific location for its accepted methods and any deposit on big installs.

## Key Concepts

- **Emergency triage**: Burst pipes, active flooding, gas smell, no heat in winter, and no cooling in extreme heat are flagged urgent and routed to the fastest available help. This is the highest-value behavior in the vertical.
- **Service-call or diagnostic fee**: A flat fee to send a technician, often credited toward the repair. Answers explain it instead of quoting a final price.
- **Service area by zip**: The AI front desk confirms coverage by city or zip before booking, since companies serve a radius rather than everywhere.
- **Estimate versus diagnostic**: Large replacements often get free estimates, while small diagnostic visits may carry a fee. The split is a tenant setting.
- **Flat-rate versus hourly**: Two common pricing models; the answer explains both and defers the exact quote to the on-site technician.
- **Maintenance plans**: Seasonal tune-ups and priority scheduling are a recurring-revenue offering many tenants run.
