Home cleaning is a recurring-revenue trade where the first questions are almost always price and frequency, so the FAQ content leads with how pricing works, the recurring-versus-one-time choice, and what separates a standard clean from a deep clean. A homeowner pricing out a weekly or biweekly service is comparing a few companies at once, and the one whose site answers the pricing and access questions clearly books the first clean. These answers are written generic so they apply across any cleaning tenant, with specific rates, packages, cancellation windows, and supply policies set during onboarding. The content here is what full-text search indexes, so each question is phrased the way a customer phrases it when they want a clean home without a phone call.

Pricing answers use ranges and "depends on the home" language because home size, number of bedrooms and bathrooms, condition, and frequency all move the number, and many companies price per visit while some price by square footage or by the hour. Recurring clients usually pay less per visit than a one-time booking, and a first deep clean often costs more than the standard cleans that follow. The access answers cover the common worry of whether the customer needs to be home, plus how keys, codes, pets, and supplies are handled, since those are the details that decide whether someone books or bounces.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How much does a cleaning cost?**
Pricing depends on the size and condition of your home, the number of bedrooms and bathrooms, and how often you want service. Many companies price per visit, while some price by square footage or by the hour. A standard clean of an average home falls in a range, and the first deep clean usually costs more. Tell us your home size and what you need and we can give a closer estimate.

**Do you charge per visit, by square footage, or by the hour?**
Pricing models vary by company. Some quote a flat price per visit based on your home, some price by square footage, and some bill by the hour plus the team size. Either way you get a quote before the clean. Tell us your home and we can explain how this company prices it.

**Do you offer recurring cleanings or just one-time?**
Most companies offer both. You can book a one-time clean or set up recurring service weekly, every two weeks, or monthly. Recurring clients usually pay a lower per-visit rate than a one-time booking because the home stays in better shape between visits. Tell us how often you want service and we can set it up.

**What is the difference between a standard clean and a deep clean?**
A standard clean covers the regular upkeep: dusting, vacuuming, mopping, surfaces, and the kitchen and bathrooms. A deep clean goes further into buildup that a standard visit does not reach, like baseboards, inside appliances, and detailed scrubbing. Many companies start new recurring clients with a deep clean, then keep it up with standard visits. We can recommend which fits your home.

**Do you do move-in or move-out cleanings?**
Many companies offer move-in and move-out cleaning for an empty or nearly empty home, which is a deeper detailed clean meant to leave the place ready for the next person or to help get a deposit back. It is priced higher than a standard clean because of the detail involved. Tell us your date and home size and we can quote it.

**Do I need to be home during the cleaning?**
You do not have to be home. Many clients give a key, a lockbox, or a door code so the team can clean while they are out. Others prefer to be home, especially for a first visit. Whatever you choose, tell us how you would like the team to get in and we will note it for your appointment.

**Do you bring your own cleaning supplies and equipment?**
Most companies bring their own supplies and equipment. If you prefer specific products, want green or fragrance-free cleaners, or have surfaces that need a particular product, tell us ahead of time and we can usually accommodate. We pass any preferences to your team.

**Do I need to do anything to get ready?**
A quick tidy of clutter and personal items helps the team focus on cleaning rather than sorting, but it is not required. Let us know about any areas to skip or focus on, and secure anything fragile or valuable you would rather handle yourself. We note your instructions for the visit.

**Are your cleaners background-checked and insured?**
Reputable cleaning companies vet their staff and carry insurance for the work. The specifics depend on the company. We can confirm the screening and coverage for the company handling your home so you feel comfortable giving access.

**What do you clean and what is not included?**
A standard clean usually covers dusting, vacuuming, mopping, surfaces, kitchen, and bathrooms. Add-ons like inside the oven, inside the fridge, interior windows, or laundry are often available for an extra charge. Some heavy or specialty tasks may be outside the scope. Tell us what you want and we can confirm what is included and what is an add-on.

**Do you clean homes with pets?**
Yes, many companies are comfortable cleaning homes with pets. Let us know what pets you have and whether they will be home or secured during the visit, so the team is prepared. Pet hair and certain areas can add time, which we factor into the estimate.

**How long does a cleaning take?**
Time depends on the size and condition of your home and the type of clean. A standard clean of an average home is often a few hours, while a deep clean or a move-out clean takes longer. We block the right amount of time when you book so the team is not rushed.

**Can I get a same-day or next-day cleaning?**
Same-day and next-day openings depend on the schedule and your area. One-time and deep cleans need a longer block, so they are easier to fit with a little notice. Tell us your home and preferred time and we can show you the soonest opening or add you to a waitlist if the day is full.

**What is your cancellation or rescheduling policy?**
Most companies ask for notice before your appointment, often 24 to 48 hours, so the slot can go to someone else or the team can adjust the route. Some charge a fee for late cancellations or lockouts. The exact window and fee depend on the company. We can tell you the policy when you book.

**What forms of payment do you take and do you accept tips?**
Most companies take cards and many take digital payments, and recurring clients are often billed automatically each visit. Tipping is appreciated but not required, and some companies let you add a tip to the bill. Check the specific company for its accepted methods.

**How do I book a cleaning?**
You can book right here in the chat, on the website booking page, or by phone during business hours. To book we confirm your home size, the type of clean, how often you want service, and an open time. You get a confirmation by text or email.

## Key Concepts

- **Per-visit, per-square-foot, or hourly pricing**: Three common models. The answer explains all three and defers the exact quote to the company once it knows the home.
- **Recurring versus one-time**: Recurring service at weekly, biweekly, or monthly cadence usually carries a lower per-visit rate than a one-time booking, and the discount is the conversion lever.
- **Deep clean versus standard**: A first deep clean handles buildup, then standard visits maintain it. New recurring clients often start with a deep clean.
- **Move-in and move-out**: A deeper detailed clean for an empty home, priced higher, tied to deposits and turnovers.
- **Access without being home**: Keys, lockboxes, and door codes let the team clean while the client is out, which removes the biggest booking objection.
- **Supplies and preferences**: Most companies bring their own supplies, with green or fragrance-free options on request, passed through to the team.
