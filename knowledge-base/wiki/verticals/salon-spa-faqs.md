## Frequently Asked Questions

**What are your hours?**
Most salons and spas are open Tuesday through Saturday, with some open on Sunday or Monday. Typical hours run from mid-morning to early evening, and many take their last appointment 60 to 90 minutes before close. Check the specific location for exact hours, since they vary by day.

**Do you take walk-ins or do I need an appointment?**
Many salons accept walk-ins when a stylist is free, but booking ahead is the safest way to get the time and stylist you want. Popular slots like Friday afternoon and Saturday fill up first. You can book online in about a minute.

**How do I book an appointment?**
You can book right here in the chat, on the website booking page, or by phone during business hours. To book, you pick the service, the stylist if you have a preference, and an open time. You get a confirmation by text or email.

**How much does a haircut cost?**
Haircut prices vary by stylist level and hair length. A basic cut commonly falls in a range, and a cut with a wash and style runs higher. Longer or thicker hair can add to the price. Ask for a quote on your specific service and we can give you a closer estimate.

**How much is color, balayage, or highlights?**
Color services vary a lot based on length, density, and how many sessions the look needs. Single-process color is the lower end, and balayage or full highlights are higher because they take more time and product. A correction or a big change from dark to light can take multiple visits. We can give a range once we know the current and target look.

**How long will my appointment take?**
A haircut is usually under an hour. Color, balayage, and highlight services often run two to four hours depending on length and the result you want. Spa services like a facial or massage are commonly 30, 60, or 90 minutes. We block the right amount of time when you book so you are not rushed.

**Can I request a specific stylist?**
Yes. When you book you can choose your preferred stylist or therapist. If they are fully booked for the day you want, we can show you their next opening or put you with another team member who does the same service.

**What is your cancellation or no-show policy?**
Most salons ask for notice before your appointment, often 24 to 48 hours, so the slot can go to someone else. Some charge a fee for late cancellations or no-shows, especially on longer color and spa bookings. The exact window and fee depend on the location.

**Do I need to leave a deposit?**
Some salons take a deposit for longer or higher-cost services like full color, extensions, or multi-hour spa packages. The deposit usually goes toward your final bill. Shorter services often do not need one. We can tell you if a deposit applies to the service you want.

**What services do you offer?**
Salons commonly offer cuts, color, highlights, balayage, blowouts, and styling. Many also do treatments, extensions, keratin, and special-occasion or bridal hair. Spas often add facials, massage, waxing, and nail services. Ask about a specific service and we can confirm it is available.

**Do you do bridal or special-occasion hair and makeup?**
Many salons offer bridal and event styling, and some take it as a package with a trial run before the day. These often need advance booking because they take longer and may involve a group. Let us know your date and party size and we can check availability.

**Are your products and services good for sensitive skin or allergies?**
Tell us about any allergies or sensitivities before your appointment. Many salons can switch to gentler or fragrance-free products, and color services may include a patch test if you have not been in before. We pass this to your stylist so they are ready.

**Do you offer gift cards?**
Many salons and spas sell gift cards for a set amount or a specific service. They make a common gift for birthdays and holidays. Ask the location how to buy one and whether it works online or in person.

**What is your first-visit or new-client process?**
For a first visit, plan to arrive a few minutes early so we can note your history and preferences. Color clients may get a quick consultation, and sometimes a patch test, before the service starts. Bring a photo if you have a look in mind, since that helps the stylist match it.

**Can I get in today or this week?**
Same-day and same-week openings depend on the day and the service. Shorter services are easier to fit in on short notice than multi-hour color. Tell us the service and your preferred time and we can show you the soonest opening or add you to a waitlist if the day is full.

**What forms of payment do you take and do you accept tips?**
Most salons take cards and many take digital payments, plus cash. Tipping is common and appreciated, and some locations let you add a tip on the card. Check the specific location for its accepted methods.

**Do you cut kids' hair, and do you do men's cuts?**
Most salons take kids and men's cuts, often at a lower price than a full-service cut. Kids' pricing usually depends on age. If you are booking for a child, mention their age and we can pick the right service and time slot.

**Do you require a deposit to book?**
Longer or high-value services like balayage, extensions, and bridal work often require a deposit that applies to your final bill. Shorter services usually book without one. The exact policy depends on the location and the service.

**Do you do bridal, prom, or event styling?**
Many salons offer updos, event styling, and bridal packages, including trials before the big day. Bridal parties can often book as a group. Tell us the date and party size and we can check availability and pricing.

**Can I book for a group or party?**
Yes, many locations take group bookings for bridal parties, birthdays, or girls' days. Groups need more chairs at once, so book as early as you can and tell us how many people and which services.

**Do you sell gift cards?**
Most salons sell gift cards or gift certificates in any amount, in person and often online. They usually work for services and retail products. Ask us and we can point you to where to buy one.

**What products do you use, and what if I have allergies or a sensitive scalp?**
Product lines vary by salon, and most carry options for sensitive skin and scalps. If you have allergies or reactions to past color services, tell us before your visit — new color clients may need a quick patch test a day or two ahead.

**Do you do extensions, keratin treatments, or perms?**
Many salons offer extensions, smoothing or keratin treatments, and perms, though not every stylist does every service. These are longer appointments and often start with a consultation so pricing depends on your hair and the result you want.

**Do you do lashes, brows, waxing, or nails?**
Full-service salons and spas often cover brows, lashes, waxing, and nail services alongside hair. Availability depends on the location's team. Tell us what you are looking for and we can confirm and book it.

**What happens if I'm running late?**
A few minutes is usually fine. Past a grace period, often 10 to 15 minutes, the salon may need to shorten the service or rebook so the next guest starts on time. If you are running behind, message or call and we will do our best to make it work.

**Is there a consultation before big changes like a color correction?**
Yes. Big changes — going much lighter, color correction, extensions, or a major restyle — usually start with a short consultation, sometimes free, so the stylist can assess your hair and give a real quote and timeline before you commit.

## Key Concepts

- **Walk-in versus appointment**: The most common first question. The answer steers the visitor toward booking, which is where the lead converts.
- **Price range language**: Salon pricing depends on length, stylist level, and product, so answers use ranges and offer a per-service quote instead of a fixed number.
- **Stylist preference**: Returning clients book by stylist, so the AI front desk supports choosing a specific team member and offering alternates when booked.
- **Cancellation window**: A 24 to 48 hour notice policy with a possible fee on longer services. The exact value is a tenant setting.
- **Deposit on high-value services**: Color, extensions, and multi-hour spa packages may require a deposit that applies to the final bill.
- **Patch test and sensitivities**: Color services for new clients may need a patch test, and the AI front desk routes allergy notes to the stylist.
