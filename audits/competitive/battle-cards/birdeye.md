# Battle Card — Birdeye (2026-04-23)

## Snapshot
**Birdeye** — reviews + reputation platform for multi-location local businesses. 3 tiers (Starter / Growth / Dominate) with prices hidden behind Configurator form. Chatbot AI gated to Dominate tier only. Estimated $300-600/mo per location.

## Tiers
| Tier | Price | Chat AI? |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | ~$300/location/mo (est) | ❌ |
| Growth | ~$450/location/mo (est) | ❌ (Social AI starts here) |
| Dominate | ~$600/location/mo (est) | ✅ (only tier with chatbot) |
| Reseller partner | sales-gated | — |

All dollar amounts estimated. Public page shows "$ /location/month" placeholder only.

## AI surface
Reviews AI, Listings AI, Messaging AI (all tiers); Social AI (Growth+); Chatbot AI (Dominate only).

## Product
- Review solicitation + response AI
- Local listings sync
- Reputation score + dashboard
- Chat widget (Dominate only)
- Surveys + insights

## Target
Multi-location local businesses: retail, auto, healthcare, dental groups. Per-location pricing assumes 3+ locations.

## Why they win (where they beat us)
- **Reviews moat** — Google/Yelp/Facebook listing management + solicitation is deep
- **Brand trust** — established incumbent with multi-year enterprise contracts
- **Multi-location fit** — per-location pricing scales with actual business growth
- **Listings sync** — SEO local search is their deep-water
- **Enterprise sales motion** — locked-in long-term contracts

## Where we beat them
| Dimension | Why we win |
|---|---|
| **Pricing transparency** | Our prices published; Birdeye gates everything behind Configurator |
| **Chat at entry tier** | Our growth $249 has AI chat; Birdeye gates chatbot to Dominate top-tier only |
| **Single-location friendliness** | Flat $249 vs Birdeye ~$300/location punishes single locations |
| **Widget-first identity** | Birdeye is reviews-first; widget is afterthought |
| **Faster evaluation cycle** | Our self-serve vs their sales call funnel |

## Discovery questions
1. "How many locations do you run?" — 1-2 locations = us; 5+ = Birdeye may fit
2. "What's the main reason you're looking — chat widget or review management?" — chat = us; reviews = Birdeye
3. "Are you willing to do a sales call to see pricing?" — no = us
4. "Do you already have review solicitation tooling?" — yes = stripping out is high friction; they may stay

## Objection handlers

### "Birdeye has better reviews management"
> Agreed. They've been at it longer and it's their primary focus. We don't try to out-reviews Birdeye. Our question: is reviews your #1 problem, or is lead capture from the website? If reviews, Birdeye wins. If chat-to-lead, we win on price and transparency.

### "We need chatbot AI — Birdeye has it"
> True — at Dominate tier, their top. Estimated $600/mo/location. Our growth at $249 ships AI chat with per-tenant KB. For a 1-location business that wants chat, we're ~3-5x cheaper.

### "Birdeye doesn't show prices because they customize per customer"
> Also true for Podium Signature, GHL Enterprise, and every enterprise software. Difference: those have published floors ($399, $497). Birdeye has nothing published. If pricing clarity matters to your evaluation, our $249/$499/$899 is on-page.

### "Birdeye is in Gartner / G2 leaders"
> Brand wins exist. Does it matter for a single-location dental office? Gartner is an enterprise signal. For SMB widget-first buyers, G2 reviews matter more, and we're building that track record now.

## Escalation signals (lean Birdeye)
- Multi-location retail or auto (5+ locations) — per-location scaling is fine
- Reviews is primary stated need
- Already on Birdeye multi-year contract — switching cost too high
- Has a dedicated marketing operator who'll do a sales call

## Signals when WE win
- Single-location SMB
- Chat widget as primary ask
- Wants to see prices without a form
- Budget conscious: wants <$400/mo total
- Has reviews tooling already (GMB, Google My Business manual)

## Imitation moves (what to steal)
1. **Reviews AI (response generation)** — add to professional tier. Deterministic template + LLM personalization. Medium effort, high perceived value.
2. **Listings sync** — too big to build; partner integration if ever needed.
3. **CSAT / NPS surveys** — low effort, medium impact; already in stickiness backlog (see `competitive-landscape-march-2026.md`).

## Proof points to cite
- Birdeye public pricing page: placeholder "$ /location/month" (confirms hidden pricing)
- Chatbot AI tier-gating: Starter/Growth = no chatbot; Dominate only
- Per-location pricing model documented on pricing page

## Watch signal
Birdeye publishing prices. If they move to transparent pricing, the wedge narrows. Check quarterly.
