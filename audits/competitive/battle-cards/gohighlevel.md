# Battle Card — GoHighLevel (2026-04-23)

## Snapshot
**GHL** — horizontal agency-reseller stack. $97-$497/mo. "AI-powered business operating system." By marketers, for marketers. ICP: marketing agencies white-labeling to SMB end-clients.

## Tiers
| Tier | Price | What unlocks |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | $97/mo | 3 sub-accounts, base features |
| Unlimited | $297/mo | unlimited sub-accounts + users |
| Agency Pro | $497/mo | **SaaS Mode** (auto sub-account provisioning), rebill-with-markup, public API |
| Enterprise | contact sales | HIPAA, dedicated CSM, white-label mobile, QBRs |

## AI surface
Voice AI, Conversation AI (chat widget), biz-card scan AI, automation AI, ad manager AI, review-response AI, prospecting AI.

## Widget positioning
Widget is **1 of 12 capture tools** — funnels, forms, surveys, calendars, reviews, voice, SMS, email, chat, ads, proposals, landing pages. Not the hook. Never pitched standalone.

## Why they win (where they beat us)
- **Broader product** — full CRM + funnels + phone + SMS
- **Agency reseller channel** — 1M+ businesses per KB, distribution flywheel via agencies
- **Lower entry** — Starter $97 vs AgentNexLiFy growth $249
- **White-label at Agency Pro** — our enterprise doesn't match this capability
- **Voice AI at every tier** — we have voice planned, not shipped

## Where we beat them
| Dimension | Why we win |
|---|---|
| **Agency-skip** | Direct-to-business buyers don't want agency middleman; GHL's ladder assumes one |
| **Widget-first identity** | Our widget is the product; theirs is 1 of 12 |
| **Per-tenant vertical KB** | GHL is generic; cannot retrofit without re-tooling agency model |
| **30-second embed claim** | GHL is 14-day funnel onboarding |
| **No CRM migration required** | GHL forces CRM adoption; we don't |
| **Flat pricing at our widget tier** | GHL Starter's $97 is attractive only if you want the bundle |

## Discovery questions (disqualify GHL fit)
1. "Do you work with a marketing agency now?" — yes = GHL is a fit
2. "Do you already have a CRM you want to keep?" — yes = GHL is friction
3. "How many businesses are you serving — your own or others?" — others = GHL; own = us
4. "When you say 'chat widget,' is that the whole thing you want, or just one piece?" — whole thing = us

## Objection handlers

### "GHL Starter is only $97"
> True — $97 gets you a CRM bundle most SMBs don't fully use, with widget buried as 1 of 12 tools. If widget is the thing you actually want, you're paying $97 for 11 tools you won't touch. Our growth at $249 is widget-first with per-tenant vertical KB — the widget knows your business, GHL's doesn't.

### "GHL has Voice AI already"
> Voice AI is table-stakes — we have it on the roadmap. The question is whether voice is the primary capture surface. For the businesses we target, website chat is where 80% of inbound lead volume originates. Voice is the upsell, not the lead.

### "GHL has white-label"
> For agencies reselling, yes. For end-businesses running their own site, white-label on someone else's back-end is not the problem — getting the widget to know your business is. That's our vertical KB per tenant.

### "GHL is more established"
> 1M+ businesses through GHL. Most of them are via agency resellers, not direct. Direct buyers who try GHL often end up paying for features they don't use. See [Podium's 1,500 leads/mo case study](../competitors-report.md#strategic-opportunities) — that's widget-volume reality, and that's our focus.

## Proof points to cite
- GHL's own site: "By marketers, for marketers" — confirms agency target
- GHL's feature list: widget listed among 12 capture tools — confirms not widget-first
- Public pricing Starter $97 with 3-sub-account cap — confirms agency economic

## Escalation signals (when customer is leaning GHL)
- Customer mentions they have or want an agency relationship → let GHL win, we're not the fit
- Customer asks about "SaaS mode" or "reselling to our clients" → not our ICP
- Customer has 5+ businesses they manage → GHL's sub-account model is right for them
