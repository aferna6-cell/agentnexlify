# Battle Card — Podium (2026-04-23)

## Snapshot
**Podium** — widget-first lead-conversion platform. $399-$599/mo + Signature contact-sales. Website chat widget on every tier. 200+ integrations. Target: 100K+ local businesses across auto, dental, HVAC, plumbing, retail. Podium Phones add-on ($25-30/user + $500/location setup). "Jerry" AI Employee addon.

Podium is the **hardest direct competitor** because their widget is real, priced, and shipped.

## Tiers
| Tier | Price | What unlocks |
|---|---|---|
| Core | $399/mo | Lead conversion, Review AI, basic automations, 200+ integrations, CSM, 250 bulk SMS credits, 5 phone numbers |
| Pro | $599/mo "Best Value" | AI Concierge, Reputation Specialist AI, AI call summaries, auto lead routing, advanced automations, lead forms, surveys, 500 SMS credits, 15 phone numbers |
| Signature | contact sales | AI Scheduler, AI Salesperson, AI Marketer, multi-location, custom billing, priority support, QBRs |
| Podium Phones | $30/user 1-4, $25/user 5+ | Phone add-on; $500/location one-time setup |
| Jerry | addon, undisclosed | AI Employee addon; price not published |

HVAC vertical: contact-sales every tier.

## AI surface
AI suggested replies, review responses, Concierge (chat AI), Reputation Specialist, call summaries, text-marketing generator, Instant Answers addon, Scheduler/Salesperson/Marketer (Signature), Jerry (addon).

## Product
- Website chat widget (core feature every tier)
- AI review responses
- Automations + lead conversion
- 200+ integrations
- Podium Phones (optional add-on)
- Surveys, bulk SMS

## Target
100K+ local businesses: auto, auto body, med spa, dental, HVAC, plumbing, jewelry, furniture, appliance, mattress, "large biz."

## Why they win (where they beat us)
- **Widget on every tier** — no gating; real widget product
- **200+ integrations** — we have few; integration breadth is real stickiness
- **Core $399 includes CSM** — onboarding hand-holding
- **Large-business case studies** — 1,500+ webchat leads/month cited
- **AI suggested replies, call summaries** — features we don't yet have
- **Podium Phones revenue** — high-margin add-on flywheel
- **Established brand** in dental, auto, HVAC

## Where we beat them
| Dimension | Why we win |
|---|---|
| **Entry price** | Growth $249 vs Podium Core $399 |
| **No CSM onboarding required** | We self-serve; they sales-call |
| **Per-tenant vertical KB** | Podium serves 10+ verticals but no per-customer KB |
| **30-second embed** | Podium is CSM-onboarded |
| **Flat vs multi-axis pricing** | Podium = tier + location + phone seats; we = tier |
| **No forced phone add-on** | Podium's revenue model pushes Phones |

## Discovery questions
1. "What's your budget per month for this?" — <$300 = us; $400+ = competitive with Podium
2. "How much time do you have to spend on onboarding?" — <30 min = us; week-long CSM = Podium
3. "Do you want a phone system too?" — yes + can afford = Podium Phones; no = we fit
4. "Do you have an existing CRM that has to integrate?" — yes with rare system = Podium's 200+ matters; no = us
5. "How many verticals / locations / brands are you running?" — single vertical = us; multiple = Podium's breadth

## Objection handlers

### "Podium has 200+ integrations"
> True. For most SMBs, the integrations that matter are 3-5: calendar, email, SMS. We have those. 200+ is impressive on a comparison matrix; irrelevant if you use 5 of them. Our per-tenant vertical KB does more work than 200 integrations — the widget actually knows your business.

### "Podium Core is $399 — close to your professional at $499"
> Close on Core vs professional, but look at what's gated. Podium gates auto lead routing, lead forms, surveys to Pro at $599. Our growth $249 has chat AI + KB. If widget + chat AI is 80% of the value, we're $150/mo cheaper with KB per tenant. If you need the CSM hand-holding and AI Concierge, Podium is the fit.

### "Podium has case studies with 1,500 leads/mo"
> Real number, real company. That's a multi-location, high-traffic site already converting well. For a single-location dental office or salon starting from scratch, that's not your comparison — your comparison is 10-50 leads/mo improvement over what your site does today. We're aligned with that starting scale.

### "Podium's AI is more mature"
> Breadth-wise, yes — Concierge, Reputation, Call Summaries, Scheduler, Salesperson, Marketer, Jerry. Depth-wise, their AI is generic; ours is per-tenant vertical KB. Your widget knows your menu / services / booking flow / pricing — Podium's doesn't.

### "Podium has Jerry — an AI Employee"
> Addon, price undisclosed. Their pattern: sell Core or Pro, then upsell Jerry via CSM. Ours: vertical KB at the widget tier, no separate AI Employee product required.

## Escalation signals (lean Podium)
- Budget >$500/mo, wants bundled phone + widget
- Multi-location, multi-vertical business
- CSM hand-holding is a desired feature, not friction
- Integration with specific 200+ list-only tool is required
- Dental or auto large-practice with existing Podium contacts

## Signals when WE win
- Self-serve buyer who doesn't want a sales call
- Single-location SMB
- Vertical we serve with KB depth (dental, legal, salon, real estate, med-spa)
- Budget <$400/mo
- Phone system already exists; no Podium Phones need

## Imitation moves (what to steal)
1. **Widget on every tier** — we already do this; amplify in marketing
2. **Case study with big volume** — we need our 1,500/mo equivalent. Pick top tenant by captured-leads, write it up.
3. **"Best Value" decoy pricing** — consider adding "Most Popular" badge to professional tier
4. **AI Concierge branding** — per-tenant AI could use a named persona (steal from Drillbit's Mason + Podium's Jerry)
5. **AI call summaries** — if/when we ship voice, add summary generation as stickiness feature

## What NOT to copy
1. **200+ integrations arms race** — we win on KB depth, not integration breadth. Don't chase.
2. **CSM-onboarded sales motion** — our self-serve motion is the moat
3. **Podium Phones** — out-of-scope; let them own phone revenue
