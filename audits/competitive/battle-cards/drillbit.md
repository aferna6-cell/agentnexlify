# Battle Card — Drillbit (2026-04-23)

## Snapshot
**Drillbit** — YC-backed single-SKU AI employee branded "Mason" for home-services and trades contractors. $500/mo per location + usage. Voice-first. No widget. Referral program, not white-label.

## Pricing
| SKU | Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Mason | $500/mo per location (base) | Usage-based after quota; no tiers; no free trial |

Guarantee: "$100 of value per $1 spent."

## Product
- Inbound phone, SMS, email handling
- Lead qualification + nurture (text / email / voice)
- Appointment booking against tech calendars
- Invoice collection
- Past-client upsell + re-engagement mining

## Channels
Phone (primary), SMS, email. No embeddable widget.

## Target
Home-services and trades contractors; franchise networks secondary. ResiBrands case study: $840K saved, 2.56x profit, $5M new revenue in 90 days.

## Why they win (where they beat us)
- **Vertical depth** — trades-specific language, integrations, calendars
- **"AI employee" framing** — sells outcome, not tool. Premium positioning justifies $500.
- **Voice-first** — correctly matches trades reality (phone is primary inbound)
- **Outcome guarantee** — "$100/$1" is a sharp marketing hook
- **YC brand + referral flywheel** — distribution via franchisors

## Where we beat them
| Dimension | Why we win |
|---|---|
| **Widget-first** | Dentists, salons, legal, med-spas get 60-80% of inbound from website, not phone |
| **Non-trade verticals** | Drillbit doesn't serve dental/legal/salon/med-spa/tutoring |
| **Lower price in our verticals** | Growth $249 vs Drillbit $500/location |
| **Flat vs per-location** | 1-location dental office pays $249 vs Drillbit $500 |
| **Per-tenant vertical KB** | Drillbit's vertical KB is trades-locked; ours is any-vertical |

## Discovery questions
1. "What industry are you in?" — home services/trades = Drillbit fits; everything else = us
2. "Where do most of your inbound leads start — phone or your website?" — phone = Drillbit; web = us
3. "How many locations do you run?" — 5+ locations in trades = Drillbit's per-location economics are fine; 1-2 locations in dental/legal = us

## Objection handlers

### "Drillbit guarantees $100 of value per $1"
> Good framing. We don't guarantee a multiple yet — we track leads captured per widget installed. Our customer data is still accumulating. If your volume is the question, we can show cumulative leads per tenant. If you want a hard guarantee, Drillbit's the right fit for trades; for dental/legal/salon/med-spa they don't serve you.

### "Mason handles phone calls; your widget doesn't"
> True. If phone is your primary inbound surface (trades, contractors), Mason is strong. For dental, legal, salon, restaurant, real estate — 60-80% of leads come from the website. We own that surface with per-tenant KB; Drillbit doesn't.

### "Drillbit is YC-backed"
> Good signal. We're not competing in trades — we've deliberately stayed out of Drillbit's lane. We pick verticals they don't touch (dental, legal, med-spa, salon, tutoring, fitness, real estate) and lead with widget, not voice.

### "$500/mo is premium — matches AI employee framing"
> Premium framing works when the outcome is direct revenue. For trades with high-ticket jobs ($2-10K avg), $500/mo pays for itself on one job. For a $50 haircut appointment at a salon, $500 is high. We size to the vertical economic, not the brand story.

## Escalation signals (when customer is leaning Drillbit)
- Home-services or trades contractor → concede; they're the right fit
- Customer says "AI receptionist" or "AI employee" > "chat widget" → language mismatch; concede if phone is primary
- Multi-location franchisor in trades → Drillbit's franchise referral wedge wins

## Signals when WE win
- Dental / legal / salon / med-spa / fitness / tutoring / real estate
- Single-location SMB
- "Chat widget" or "website chat" in customer language
- Budget conscious: wants <$300/mo
- Already has phone system; doesn't want to rip it out

## Imitation moves (what to steal)
1. **Single-SKU simplicity marketing** — their page is clean. Our pricing page has 4 tiers + legacy; could A/B a simplified page per vertical landing.
2. **Outcome-based tagline** — "$100 per $1" is a benchmark. Once we have per-tenant aggregated lead data, publish our own ratio. Example: "3 leads captured per $10 spent."
3. **"Mason" as character branding** — naming the agent humanizes it. We could name per-tenant agents (e.g. "Ava, your dental front desk").

## Proof points to cite
- Drillbit site confirms single-vertical focus: home services / trades / franchises
- Drillbit has no embeddable widget; voice + SMS + email only
- Drillbit's "$100/$1" is a marketing claim, not a contractual guarantee
