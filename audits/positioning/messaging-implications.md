# Messaging Implications — AgentNexLiFy (2026-04-23)

Word-level language map derived from positioning. Use these in marketing copy, sales emails, pricing page, in-product tooltips.

---

## Words to USE (positive language anchors)

### Product identity
- **Widget** (not "chatbot", not "conversational agent")
- **Chat** (not "messaging", not "dialog")
- **AI** (default in 2026; avoid "artificial intelligence" long form)
- **Vertical** (as in "vertical AI" — signals per-tenant KB)
- **Embed** (signals quick install)
- **Script tag** (technical buyer); **one-line install** (non-technical)

### Setup / install
- **30 seconds** (specific time claim)
- **One script tag** (concrete unit)
- **Embed today** (imperative urgency)
- **No CRM migration** (negative positive)
- **No agency** (negative positive)
- **Self-serve** (empowerment frame)

### Value prop
- **Knows your business** (core positioning phrase)
- **Your menu / your services / your pricing** (specificity)
- **Trained on your business** (vertical KB)
- **Captures leads** (outcome)
- **Books appointments** (outcome)
- **Follows up** (automation value)
- **Answers 24/7** (availability)

### Pricing language
- **Flat** (vs per-location)
- **Published** (transparency signal)
- **No Configurator** (Birdeye jab)
- **No sales call** (self-serve signal)
- **$249 to start** (specific number, no "starting at")
- **Free tier** (try-before-buy frame)

### Buyer audience signals
- **Small business** (SMB, not "SMB" abbreviation unless industry publication)
- **Local business** (when physical location matters)
- **Single location** (when anti-Podium/Birdeye)
- **1-3 locations** (specific band)
- **Independent practice / shop / firm / studio** (vertical-specific)

## Words to AVOID (anti-language)

### Marketing fluff
- ❌ "unlock" / "unleash" / "leverage" / "empower" / "synergy" / "best-in-class" / "cutting-edge" / "revolutionary"
- ❌ "seamless" (meaningless; describe specifically)
- ❌ "powerful" (say what it does)
- ❌ "robust" (technical-speak)
- ❌ "world-class" (hollow superlative)
- ❌ "game-changer" / "disrupt"
- ❌ "next-generation" / "NextGen"

### Wrong category language
- ❌ "Chatbot" (older framing — use "AI chat" or "AI chat widget")
- ❌ "Conversational AI platform" (too enterprise)
- ❌ "Customer service" (we're lead capture; different use case)
- ❌ "Business operating system" (GHL's framing; don't compete there)
- ❌ "CRM" (we are NOT a CRM; explicitly "lives next to your CRM")
- ❌ "AI receptionist" (Drillbit's framing; voice-first)
- ❌ "Lead generation software" (generic; use "lead capture widget")

### Non-ICP signals
- ❌ "Enterprise" (we're SMB; using "enterprise" confuses ICP)
- ❌ "Scale" (not the buyer's concern)
- ❌ "Multi-tenant" (technical; buyer doesn't care)
- ❌ "Franchise" (Drillbit's target; not ours)
- ❌ "Agency-ready" / "White-label" (GHL's target)

### Vague benefit language
- ❌ "Save time" (say how much)
- ❌ "Increase conversions" (say from what to what)
- ❌ "Better customer experience" (define it)
- ❌ "Boost revenue" (marketing-sales-slide speak)

## Home-page copy map

### Hero (above the fold)
- **H1:** category statement with theme A value
  → "The AI chat widget that actually knows your business."
- **Sub:** theme B install frame + theme C pricing
  → "Install in 30 seconds. No CRM migration. No agency. Per-tenant knowledge base trained on your services, pricing, and hours. Flat $249/mo. Free to try."
- **CTA:** imperative verb, 1-3 words
  → Primary: "Embed free" · Secondary: "See pricing"

### Social proof block (below hero)
- Specific outcome numbers (not "loved by thousands")
- Logos of verticals we serve (dental, salon, legal, etc.)
- Pull-quote from tenant with attribution

### Value themes section (3 cards)
1. **Card 1 — Theme A:** "Knows your business from day one"
   - Copy: "Your widget learns from a per-tenant knowledge base — your menu, your services, your pricing. Not a generic template."
2. **Card 2 — Theme B:** "Installed in 30 seconds"
   - Copy: "One script tag. No CRM migration. No agency. No CSM call."
3. **Card 3 — Theme C:** "Honest, flat pricing"
   - Copy: "$249, $499, $899. On the page. Same for 1 location as for 3."

### Comparison table (inline, not hidden)
- Rows: Features (widget on every tier, per-tenant KB, transparent pricing, flat pricing, no CRM migration, self-serve, 30-second install)
- Columns: AgentNexLiFy, Podium, Birdeye, GoHighLevel
- Use ✅ / ⚠️ / ❌ clearly

### FAQ (key questions)
- "How is this different from Podium?"
- "Do I need to replace my CRM?"
- "How long does install actually take?"
- "What does 'vertical' mean in 'vertical AI chat widget'?"
- "Can I see pricing without a sales call?"

## Internal writing discipline

- **Always ground claims in numbers.** Not "fast install" — "30 seconds."
- **Always name the vertical when possible.** Not "small business" — "dental practice."
- **Always name the anti-pattern.** Not "better than competitors" — "no Configurator form, no CSM call."
- **Never use em dashes as filler.** Use them to separate — or convert to sentences.
- **Never open with "Just" or "Simply."** Both weaken the statement.

## Brand voice notes (cross-ref `.claude/rules/personality.md`)

- Direct, evidence-first, no preamble
- Avoids marketing speak
- Concrete numbers beat adjectives
- Technical precision over politeness
- Owns conclusions, doesn't hedge
- Short sentences, short paragraphs

Apply these to every piece of copy. See `design.md` "Writing Tone" section for UI copy discipline.
