## 1. Moore Template (for internal strategy doc / investor decks)

> **For** 1-3 location small businesses in dental, legal, salon, med-spa, real estate, restaurant, auto, medical, and tutoring verticals
> **Who** need AI-powered lead capture on their existing website without migrating to a new CRM or hiring a marketing agency,
> **AgentNexLiFy** is a **vertical AI chat widget**
> **That** ships with a per-tenant knowledge base, installs in 30 seconds, and publishes flat, transparent pricing.
> **Unlike** GoHighLevel (agency stack with widget buried among 11 other tools), Podium (widget-strong but $399+ with CSM onboarding), and Birdeye (hides pricing and gates chatbot to top tier),
> **Our product** knows each customer's specific business from day one and requires no CRM migration, no agency middleman, and no sales call to buy.

---

## 2. Neumeier Onliness Test

**"The only [X] that [Y]."**

> AgentNexLiFy is the only **AI chat widget for small business** that **ships with a per-tenant vertical knowledge base — so the widget knows the customer's specific menu, services, and pricing from the moment it's embedded.**

Variant with broader framing:
> AgentNexLiFy is the only **small-business AI widget** that **lives next to your existing stack (no CRM migration), installs in 30 seconds, and prices flat — with a knowledge base trained on YOUR business, not a generic template.**

Both pass the Onliness filter: swap any competitor's name in and the sentence becomes false.

## 3. Elevator pitch (30 seconds)

> Small businesses lose most of their website leads to slow follow-up or broken contact forms. Podium and GoHighLevel will sell them a chat widget, but it takes a CSM call, an agency, or a CRM migration to install. **AgentNexLiFy is the vertical AI chat widget that installs in 30 seconds with a knowledge base trained on your specific business** — your dental practice, your salon, your law firm. Flat pricing on-page, $249/mo, no per-location gouging. We don't try to be your CRM; we catch the leads your CRM is missing.
